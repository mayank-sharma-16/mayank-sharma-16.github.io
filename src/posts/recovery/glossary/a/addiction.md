# History and Etymology

*"One of the defining features of addiction is an impairment of self-control: addicts are people who (to all appearance) find it especially difficult to bring their behavior into line with their best judgments. Addicts often report a loss of control, and theorists have often used the language of loss of control to describe addiction."* - Neil Levy, *Addiction and Self-Control: Perspectives from Philosophy, Psychology, and Neuroscience*
