In 1928, the American Public Health Association (APHA) combined The Nation's Health with the American Journal of Public Health (AJPH) in an attempt to have a single comprehensive platform for research, policy, and news. In 1971, this had proved to be an unwieldy task, so they once again split into the AJPH and The Nation's Health.
