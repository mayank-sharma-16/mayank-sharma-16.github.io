# Abstract

*"Particularly concerning was the finding that mental professionals held significantly higher stigmatizing beliefs, highlighting that outreach to health clinics, mental health centers, pharmacies, and related agencies is needed in community-based anti-stigma campaigns"*

In other words, the mental health workers responsible for helping those struggling with addiction had more stigma than the general population.
