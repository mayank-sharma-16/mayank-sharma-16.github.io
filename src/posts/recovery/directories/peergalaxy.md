# Overview
Peer Galaxy is a platform that aggregates peer-led recovery services and events across the world with a focus on Oregon. From their main page, they maintain peer support, recovery and wellness activities, webinars, consumer input, and special events. They also seem to have an appreciable focus on addressing barriers to participation through accessibility and translation options that are often a secondary consideration for both online and offline recovery groups.
