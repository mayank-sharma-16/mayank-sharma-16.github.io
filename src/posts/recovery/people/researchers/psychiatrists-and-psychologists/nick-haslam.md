# Works
- [Concept Creep and the Mental Health Crisis](https://spssi.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/sipr.70007)

*"I argue that rising attention to harm, coupled with the pervasive influence of the mental health industry, has led to concepts of mental health and illness becoming increasingly expansive. This process of “concept creep” promotes the adoption of diagnostic identities and a tendency to over-pathologize experience and behavior. These trends not only increase the number of people who are identified as having mental health problems and seek treatment for them but may also worsen mental health through a range of self-fulfilling and iatrogenic processes."*
