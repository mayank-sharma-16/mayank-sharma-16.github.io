---
title: William F. Bynum
---

William Bynum (also referred to often as W.F. Bynum) is a notable historian whose 1968 paper pointed out the way acute impacts of alcoholism or heavy drinking were acknowledged by medicinal authorities, but the concept of "chronic alcoholism" still seemed ill-defined and elusive. He collaborated with another notable historian of medicine, Roy Porter, on the Companion Encyclopedia of the History of Medicine (1994)
