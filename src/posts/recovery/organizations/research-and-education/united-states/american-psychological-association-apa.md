# Overview

With over 190,000 members, the American Psychological Association (APA) is the largest psychology association in the world. 

Students who grew up in the American education system are likely to have been taught about the MLA style guide for formatting writing. The APA style guide is an alternative that many students see but haven't been introduced to unless they spend time specifically exploring social science literature. 

# Alumni

## Presidents

Like many other professional and research organizations, the APA President is elected to a one-year tenure. Only Granville Stanley Hall and William James have been exceptions to this as they were elected before the one-year limit was in place.

- Granville Stanley Hall (1892)

- William James (1894)

- Hugo Munsterberg (1898)

- John Dewey (1899)

- William James (1904)

- Edward Lee Thorndike (1912)
