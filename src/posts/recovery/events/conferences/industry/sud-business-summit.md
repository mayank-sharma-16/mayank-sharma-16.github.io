# Overview
The SUD Business Summit is hosted by the media platform Behavioral Health Business (BHB), designed for corporations to discuss the economics and investment landscape of the addiction and recovery industry.

# Attendees

- David Gomel, CEO of Rosecrance Behavioral Health

- Jaime Vinck, President of Meadows Behavioral Health

- Doug Leech, Founder and CEO of Ascension Recovery Services

- Michael Garafalo, Directory of Behavioral Health at Fallon Health

- Chuck Steiner, Senior Executive at Bradford Health Services
