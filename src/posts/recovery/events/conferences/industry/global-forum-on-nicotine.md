# Overview
Founded in 2014 by KAC Communications, the Global Forum on Nicotine conference is held in Warsaw, Poland with a hybrid format using both on-site presenters and online viewers. They hold open debates on tobacco harm reduction (THR). They also host a GFN Film Festival showcasing documentaries, animations, and PSAs about tobacco and nicotine. The Michael Russell Oration and Award is given to leaders in the field of THR.
