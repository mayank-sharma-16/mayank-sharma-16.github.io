# Overview
Hosted by the Consortium on Addiction Recovery Science (CoARS) since 2024, the National Conference on Addiction Recovery Science (NCARS) claims to be the only national conference exclusively focused on the science of substance use disorder recovery. It was designed as a virtual-first event to maximize accessibility and input into discussions about measuring recovery capital rather than just abstinence or harm reduction.

# Attendees

- George Koob, Director of the National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism (NIAAA)

- William White

- Tom Coderre, then-Acting Principal Deputy Secretary for the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA)

- Tisha Wiley, Chief of the Services Research Branch at NIDA

- Laura Kwako, Health Scientist Administrator at NIAAA
