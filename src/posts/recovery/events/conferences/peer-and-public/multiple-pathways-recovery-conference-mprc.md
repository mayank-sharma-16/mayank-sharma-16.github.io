# Overview
Though established in 2015 by the Connecticut Community for Addiction Recovery (CCAR), the Multiple Pathways of Recovery Conference (MPRC) is a national conference recognizing successful pathways to recovery using secular, clinical, holistic, or other programs outside the traditional 12-step focus. More recently, CCAR has invited collaboration over hosting the event to incorporate recovery coaches, clinical professionals, and regional organizations with data-sharing and advocacy strategies. 

# Attendees

- William L. White, author of *Slaying the Dragon: The History of Addiction Treatment and Recovery in America*

- Tommy Rosen, founding of Recovery 2.0

- Don Coyhis, founder of White Bison, Inc. and pioneer of the Wellbriety Movement

- Mark Lundholm, a stand-up comedian and author
