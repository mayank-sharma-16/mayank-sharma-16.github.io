Directory

Manknotes is ad-free, subscription-free, and tracking-free thanks to support of every contributor in this directory.

The goal of this directory is to aggregate resources useful to people in all stages of recovery.

We prioritize listings from people in recovery themselves. Recovery has always relied on mutual aid and peer support instead of waiting for someone to save us. We intend to honor that premise.

Listings are not endorsements. We believe in informed choices, so this site has information, but we can't know what choice is best for you. Take what works, leave what doesn't.
