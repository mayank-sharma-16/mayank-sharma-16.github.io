# About MankNotes
I read a lot, and I write a lot. Some of it might be useful to people. I aim to keep this as accessible as possible. I will never have a paywall or ask for a subscription to anything here. Gatekeeping knowledge like that is abhorrent to my being. It's up there with hoarding land. No promises about ads, though. I still need to pay rent.

Addiction is fascinating. I haven't found anything in the world that's more interdisciplinary as an intellectual project. The history of drug use alone is worthy of a lifetime of study. Behind every approach to addiction and recovery is a theory of suffering, and behind each theory of suffering is a theory of the human condition. Finding a sufficient theory is a matter of life, death, or a life of wishing for death. Every social institution has completely fumbled their approach to addressing addiction at some point in the last century, and in each failure to enact meaningful reform, we realize how little we as a species know about ourselves. 

I don't find addiction to be embarrassing. I find it embarrassing to be part of a society that can't address it. We have common-sense solutions, but we seem to be either too stupid, too greedy, or too complacent to do them.
