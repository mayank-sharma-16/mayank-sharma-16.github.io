# Overview
Hedonism is a philosophy that places pleasure as the highest goal of life. 

Psychological hedonism is a descriptive theory that posits humans are driven by the desire to maximize pleasure while minimizing pain, similar to Freud's idea of the pleasure principle.

Ethical hedonism is a prescriptive theory that people should place pleasure as their highest goal in life and that pleasure is inherently valuable.
