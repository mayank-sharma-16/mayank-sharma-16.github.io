# Overview
A Bodhisattva is a Buddhist practitioner who has taken a commitment to delay their own liberation through enlightenment into Nirvana until every other living being has also been able to achieve this liberation. This generally involves taking a vow to demonstrate that one has cultivated [[bodhicitta|bodhicitta]], or the heart-mind of an enlightened being. 

Mahayana and Zen Buddhism put forward four vows for Bodhisattvas to take:

1. Beings are numberless. I vow to save them.

2. Delusions are inexhaustible. I vow to end them.

3. Dharma gates are boundless. I vow to enter them.

4. The Buddha's way in unsurpassable. I vow to attain it.
