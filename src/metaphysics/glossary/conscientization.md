# Overview
Conscientization is the process through which individuals develop the critical consciousness required to participate in shaping the social, political, economic systems that power dynamics exist in. 

# History and Etymology
The modern usage of the term was popularized by Paulo Freire in *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* (1970) which discussed the ways that individuals facing oppressive systems that marginalize them moved from passive dispositions or learned helplessness to become actively self-empowered within the systems they live in.
