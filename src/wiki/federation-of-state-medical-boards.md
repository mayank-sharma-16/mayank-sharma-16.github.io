# Overview
The Federation of State Medical Boards (FSMB) is a non-profit founded in 1912 that represents the over 70 medical regulatory boards across the United States that manage physician credentialing. 

They work closely with the Federation of State Physician Health Programs (FSPHP). Physician Health Programs (PHPs) are confidential programs where a physician can seek treatment for [[substance-use-disorder|substance use disorders]] without fear of punishment or disciplinary consequences. The FSMB House of Delegates maintains that licensees should abstain from using marijuana while practicing medicine because the cognitive impairments are difficult to measure.

They also update a framework for physicians managing controlled substances called the "Model Policy for the Use of Opioid Analgesics in the Treatment of Chronic Pain." [^0]
