# Overview
The Canadian Centre on Substance Use and Addiction (CCSA) is an independent non-government organization created in 1988 through an Act of Parliament. The CCSA conducts and analyzes scientific research to give evidence-based recommendations to policymakers and decision-makers, ideally remaining as neutral and objective as possible for a government-created institution subject to political approval. Most recently, the CCSA has been working on the Small Cities Playbook for Action, the Canadian Community Epidemiology Network on Drug Use (CCENDU), and National Guidance for Youth Substance Use Health.
