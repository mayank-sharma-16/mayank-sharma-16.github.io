title: Law of Attraction
---

# Overview
The law of attraction is a spiritual or religious belief that the thoughts or attitude one has invites corresponding events; if one maintains positive thinking, then positive experiences will happen, but if one maintains negative thinking, then negative experiences will happen.

# Commentary
Of all the New Age bullshit in what passes for "spirituality" in the modern world, the law of attraction is one of the biggest agitators of my nerves.

First, it's not a real "law" in any sense of the world. It's pseudoscience. There is no consistent body of empirical evidence that suggests that believing in your ability to manifest positive outcomes actually leads to positive outcomes.

From a 2023 paper published in *Society for Personality and Social Psychology*:

*We explored the psychology of those who believe in manifestation: the ability to cosmically attract success in life through positive self-talk, visualization, and symbolic actions... Those who scored higher on the scale perceived themselves as more successful, had stronger aspirations for success, and believed they were more likely to achieve future success. They were also more likely to be drawn to risky investments, have experienced bankruptcy, and to believe they could achieve an unlikely level of success more quickly. We discuss the potential positives and negatives of this belief system in the context of growing public desire for success and an industry that capitalizes on these desires.*

In other words, people who believe their thoughts could manifest positive outcomes certainly believed they were bound to have more positive outcomes, but in fact were more prone to suffering the consequences of greater risk-taking and loftier ambitions. 

Where there is some data to back the idea of "positive thinking" or visualizing what you want to manifest can be found in the distinction between process visualization and outcome visualization. In other words, imagining that you'll be successful as long as you have a positive attitude doesn't help, but imagining yourself doing the actual steps required for success does seem to help, likely because you're priming your brain for the task by retrieving relevant information and preparing a strategy to execute. That's not the same as changing reality through a positive attitude though. 

Second, it really easily lends itself to the suppression and stifling of anything that can be perceived or felt negatively. If someone has complaints about negative events or influences in their life, the law of attraction implies that this person has a negative attitude that's responsible for their outcomes, which completely reverses the order of events in reality. People who undergo negative experiences are understandably going to have negative expectations of the world as well, or at least a negative bias relative to others who had more positive experiences. Giving them the responsibility of their own pain and suffering can often cascade into victim-blaming.
