# Overview
Rural Addiction Prevention (RAP) is a nonprofit dedicated to preventing and treating [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] in rural Alabama. Their spaces provide services like community education and free [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] as [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]].
