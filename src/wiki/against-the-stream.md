# Overview
Against the Stream is an American Buddhist movement founded by Noah Levine in 2007 that blends Theravada Buddhist foundations with Gen-X rebellious attitudes. Levine claims Buddhist [[meditation|meditation]] practices saved him from a life of [[addiction|addiction]] and crime as a form of subverting social standards that worked against the normalization of greed, hatred, delusion, and identification with ego.
