# Overview
Zen Nihon Danshu Renmei, known in English as All Nippon Abstinence Association, is the largest network of self-help groups for those struggling with alcohol [[dependence|dependence]] in Japan outside of Alcoholics Anonymous (AA). They provide [[peer-recovery|peer support networks]], recovery group meetings, and [[rehab|rehabilitation programs]].

# Historical Context
The organization was formed in 1963 after consolidating local [[danshukai|Danshukai]] groups that formed in Tokyo in 1958.
