# Overview
The University of Alabama at Birmingham established the UAB Medicine Addiction Recovery Program in 1994, which runs out of the UAB Hospital and includes inpatient [[detoxification|detoxification]], intensive outpatient programs (IOP), specialized counseling with modalities like Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR), and structured aftercare for two years for long-term [[recovery|recovery]].
