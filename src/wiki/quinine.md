# Overview
Quinine is a naturally occurring alkaloid found in the cinchona tree, and it's primarily used as a prescription medication that treats malaria. Although quinine isn't a controlled substance itself, it's commonly used as a "cutting agent" that contaminates street drugs like heroin and cocaine due to its similar texture and bitter flavor profile. It also tends to compound the stimulant effects of cocaine to make the effects feel more [[potency|potent]], or cause a rapid drop in blood pressure when injected alongside heroin that can feel like a rush.
