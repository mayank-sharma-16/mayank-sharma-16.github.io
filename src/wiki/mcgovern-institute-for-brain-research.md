# Overview
The McGovern Institute of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology has dedicated projects for research into [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]] from a [[neurobiology|neurobiological]] perspective. Other topics include [[optogenetics|optogenetics]], [[cannabis|cannabis]] impact studies, and the neuroscience of cravings.
