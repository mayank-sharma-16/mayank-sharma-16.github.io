# Overview
Tecato gusano translates to "junkie worm" as Spanish slang that serves as a metaphor for the internal urges and cravings that drive someone to use heroin, and it emerged in the culture of Southern California Chicanos who used to struggle with substance use. It's similar to the English idiom about having a "monkey on your back." The problem of recovery is often described as needing to keep the worm asleep by avoiding cues that trigger cravings, reminding oneself of the consequences of relapse, and social support networks.
