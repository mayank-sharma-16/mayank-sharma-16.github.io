# Overview
The Johns Hopkins Bayview Medical Center (JHBMC) is the primary base for the Johns Hopkins Center for Substance Abuse Treatment and Research. Their services include:

- Addiction Medicine Units (AMUs): specialized inpatient units to manage [[detoxification|detoxifiation]] and [[withdrawal|withdrawal]] 

- Addiction Treatment Services (ATS): outpatient care that integrates psychiatric therapy with Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) that uses [[methadone|methadone]] and [[buprenorphine|buprenorphine]].

- Center for Addiction and Pregnancy (CAP): an interdisciplinary program designed for pregnant and postpartum women who struggle with [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]]

- Behavioral Pharmacology Research Unit (BPRU): a human laboratory conducting clinical trials on how drugs affect the brain and behavior

- hosting campuses of the National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA) and National Institute on Aging (NIA)
