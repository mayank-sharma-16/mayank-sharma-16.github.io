# Overview
The Jamie Daniels Foundation is a non-profit based in Michigan that supports youth and young adults with [[substance-use-disorder|substance use disorder]]. It was established in 2018 by hockey announcer Ken Daniels and Lisa Daniels-Goldman to honor the memory of their son Jamie, who passed away at 23 from a [[fentanyl|fentanyl]]-laced pill while in a [[rehab|rehabilitation]] facility. Their key initiatives include funding for collegiate [[recovery|recovery]] networks, preventative programming in elementary schools involving healthy coping skills, and hosting fundraising events like an annual celebrity roast.
