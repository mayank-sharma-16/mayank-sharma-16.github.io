# Overview
Psychoterratic refers to mental and emotional states experienced in connection with the state of the environment and Earth as a whole. This includes:

- anxiety and grief caused from the threats to ecological stability

- solastalgia, or the distress experienced when someone feels a familiar home environment being altered around them.

- terrafurie, or the state of anger directed at systems perceived to be threatening the environment

- topophilia, or a positive bond someone has to a place 

- soliphilia, or the political solidarity needed to protect a shared environment
