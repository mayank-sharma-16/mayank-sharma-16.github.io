# Overview
Remission describes a state when symptoms of a disease decrease or disappear. It's a narrower concept than [[recovery|recovery]], which describes the entire process of healing towards an improved quality of life.

In the DSM-5-TR, remission for substance use disorder (SUD) means that someone who used to meet its diagnostic criteria no longer meets any except cravings. Early remission is considered the period between 3 months and 12 months without meeting any SUD criteria, while sustained remission is used to describe the period of time after 12 months.
