# Overview
Neuroanatomy is the study of the structure of the nervous system, generally organized into three systems: the Central Nervous System (CNS), Peripheral Nervous System, and Autonomic Nervous System.

The CNS includes the brain and spinal cord, which means it's primarily responsible for processing sensory data and issuing motor commands to the body. The brain is split into four lobes: frontal, parietal, temporal, and occipital. It also includes the cerebellum and brainstem.
