# Overview
Tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) is a psychoactive compound found in cannabis. It binds to receptors in the endocannabinoid system to alter mood, memory, perception, appetite, heart rate, etc. 

Consumption methods of THC include smoking, vaporizing, edibles, infused beverages, capsules and tablets, [[sublingual|sublingual]] tinctures, oral sprays, creams, lotions, transdermal patches, and others. There's a thriving set of subcultures around the world wrapped around cannabis usage that are constantly experimenting with new methods and strains.
