# Overview
Snuff is a type of tobacco made from finely ground tobacco leaves that is then inhaled through the nose or placed inside the mouth. More than 80% of smokeless tobacco users live in South and Southeast Asia, particularly India and Bangladesh.

Short-term effects of snuff are similar to those of other tobacco products:

- Elevated heart rate and blood pressure

- Tissue inflammation and irritation

- Anxiety and irritability, between doses

- Nausea and stomach irritation

- Dizziness

The same applies to long-term effects:

- Increased risk of cancers of the oral cavity, tongue, gum, cheek, esophagus, pancreas, and other organs

- Receding gums and bone loss around dental roots

- Increased risk of coronary heart disease, atherosclerosis, and ischemic stroke

- Increased risk of stillbirth or premature delivery
