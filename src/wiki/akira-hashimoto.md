# Overview
Akira Hashimoto is a Japanese medical historian and professor at Aichi Prefectural University with a focus on the history of psychiatry and mental health policies, including welfare sciences and substance use programs. His recent projects include comparing the development of drug treatment and [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] frameworks across Australia, Switzerland, and Germany in comparison to Japan's [[abstinence|abstinence]]-focused policies.
