# Overview
Amy Winehouse was a widely celebrated English pop singer whose struggles with substance use were widely covered through the media. She died from [[alcohol|alcohol]] poisoning after years of escalating mental health issues and rehab attempts following the death of her grandmother in 2005. She was hospitalized in 2007 for overdosing on a combination of [[heroin|heroin]], [[cocaine|cocaine]], [[ketamine|ketamine]], [[alcohol|alcohol]], and [[mdma|MDMA (ecstacy)]]. Although she had entered high levels of institutional care and undergone crisis interventions multiple times before, she was repeatedly resistant to formal psychiatric treatment and medication out of fear that it would dull her edge and creativity.
