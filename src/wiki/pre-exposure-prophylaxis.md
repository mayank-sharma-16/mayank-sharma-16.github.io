# Overview
Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) is a prescription medication taken to prevent the recipience of HIV from sex or injection drug use. It can come in the form of daily pills and long-acting injections to be taken twice a year. If someone has recently been exposed to a risk factor for HIV or bacterial STIs, they may first undergo a [[post-exposure-prophylaxis|post-exposure prophylactic (PEP)]] treatment designed for short-term recent exposure before undergoing PrEP for long-term prevention.
