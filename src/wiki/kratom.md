# Overview
Kratom refers to the Mitragyna speciosa tree or its leaves that produce psychoactive effects. It has a biphasic profile because it acts similar to a stimulant in low doses, but it acts closer to a sedative in high doses. The active alkaloids in kratom include mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH), which both bind to the body's [[endogenous|endogenous]] opioid receptors, but in a different way than well-known [[exogenous|exogenous]] opioids like morphine or heroin, which are full agonists. Kratom alkaloids are partial agonists that don't bind as strongly to opioid receptors.
