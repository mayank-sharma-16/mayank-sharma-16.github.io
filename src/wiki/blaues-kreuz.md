# Overview
Blaues Kreuz (Blue Cross) is an international Christian non-governmental organization (NGO) focused on addiction prevention and treatment. It was founded in Switzerland in 1877 and grew to over touch over 40 to 50 countries under the International Blue Cross (IBC) umbrella organization. Their programs include interactive workshops at schools, alcohol-free cocktail bars, workplace health consultations to help employees struggling with [[dependence|dependence]], outpatient clinics, counseling sessions, [[rehab|rehabilitation centers]], [[peer-recovery|peer support groups]], sober recreation and retreats. As a whole, the organization aims at encouraging people to achieve [[abstinence|abstinence]].
