# Overview
Sublingual refers to the place in the mouth under the tongue. Many medications are taking sublingually to bypass the digestive tract and liver metabolism to be absorbed directly into the bloodstream. This means they can begin working just a few minutes after they've been taken.

Examples of medications taken sublingually include:

- Nitroglycerin: chest pain

- Buprenorphine: [[opioid-use-disorders|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]]

- Allergy medication

- Vitamin supplements
