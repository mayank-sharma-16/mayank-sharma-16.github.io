# Overview
Syringe services programs (SSPs) provide sterilized needles and safe disposal options for those who inject substances. In the United Kingdom, the term Needle and Syringe Programmes (NSPs) might be used instead. As a [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] public health approach, they also tend to be provided alongside other services like:

- [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] distribution

- [[fentanyl-test-strips|Fentanyl test strips]] 

- Vaccines for [[sexually-transmitted-infections|STIs]], like HIV, hepatitis B, and hepatitis C

- Referrals to mental health care and [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] treatment

According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), SSPs reduce the [[prevalence|prevalence]] of HIV and hepatitis C by 50% without increasing the rate of substance use or crimes.
