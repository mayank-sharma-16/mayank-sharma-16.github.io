# Overview
Zoopharmacognosy is the study of how animals self-medicate to address their illnesses. 

As examples:

- Chimpanzees swallow leaves to purge stomach parasites 

- Capuchin monkeys use limes and onions to repel insects

- Crows flatten themselves over anthills to let ants climb through their feathers, since the ants release formic acid which destroys mites, lice, and fungi

- Dolphins rub their skin against corals and sea sponges that release antimicrobial mucus and protects against infections through the skin
