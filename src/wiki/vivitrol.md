# Overview
Vivitrol is a prescription medication that contains naltrexone and is used to treat Alcohol Use Disorder (AUD) and [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]]. It's administered as an [[intramuscular|intramuscular]] injection once a month to block the receptors in the brain that produce the rewarding effects of alcohol and opioids.
