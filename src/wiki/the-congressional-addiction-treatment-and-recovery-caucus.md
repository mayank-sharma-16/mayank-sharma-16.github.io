# Overview
The Congressional Addiction, Treatment, and Recovery (ATR) Caucus is a bipartisan group of over 80 members in Congress that was the first to recognize [[addiction|addiction]] as a disease. The legislation they've led includes:

- Mainstream Addiction Treatment (MAT) Act: allows all [[drug-enforcement-agency|DEA]]-registered healthcare providers to prescribe buprenorphine for [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]] 

- SUPPORT for Patients and Communities Act: expands Medicaid coverage for [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] treatment, protects coverage for youth involved in the justice system, and creating projects to increase treatment capacity

- Addiction Treatment Access Improvement Act: expands the type of healthcare practitioners who can prescribe medication-assisted treatment (MAT) for addiction
