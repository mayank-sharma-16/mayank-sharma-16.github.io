# Overview
Protective factors are attributes that promote resilience and well-being, reducing the impact of risk factors. 

The Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) uses the Socio-Ecological Model (SEM) to assess risk and protect factors across four levels:

- Individual: biological and psychological traits

- Relationship: closest social circles like family and peers

- Community: settings where social relationships can start and develop

- Societal: cultural, economic, and political factors
