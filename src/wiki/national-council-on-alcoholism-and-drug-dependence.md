# Overview
The National Council on Alcoholism and Drug Dependence was founded by [[marty-mann|Marty Mann]] to address [[alcoholism|alcoholism]] through campaigns arguing that it was [[a-disease-like-any-other|"a disease like any other,"]] making it the oldest nonprofit advocacy organization for [[addiction|addiction]] in the United States.
