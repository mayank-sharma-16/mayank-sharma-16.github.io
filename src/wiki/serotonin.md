# Overview
Serotonin is a neurotransmitter produced by the gut to regulate happiness, anxiety, sleep-wake cycles, digestion and nausea, bone density, libido, and other bodily functions.

Low serotonin levels have been linked to depression, [[anxiety|anxiety]], disrupted sleep, and digestion problems. Excessive serotonin levels can cause serotonin syndrome, which can happen as the result of mixing medications and substances.
