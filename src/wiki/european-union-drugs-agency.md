# Overview
The European Union Drugs Agency (EUDA) is the official agency of the European Union in charge of providing information about illicit drugs across the continent. It was originally called the European Monitoring Centre for Drugs and Drug Addiction (EMCDDA), but in 2024, it transitioned into EUDA as part of a shift into being a service-oriented organization rather than just an information hub. Their model is "anticipate, alert, respond, and learn."

Their recent projects include:

- European Drug Alert System: a 24/7 early warning framework to coordinate forensic lab data and deliver security alerts when [[designer-drug|synthetic drugs]], [[novel-psychoactive-substances|new psychoactive substances (NPS)]], hazardous [[contaminant|contaminants]], and precursor manufacturing trends are identified

- EUDA4Peru: an operation to integrate European intelligence with South American law enforcement near the origin points of cocaine trafficking routes to prevent commercial container shipping hubs from being exploited by cartels

- European Drug Report: an annual publication issued by EUDA as a definitive reference for the illicit drug market in Europe and its influence on public health outcomes
