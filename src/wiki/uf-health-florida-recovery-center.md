# Overview
The University of Florida Health's Florida Recovery Center is a specialized mental health treatment facility that addresses [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] alongside other disorders. Their treatment options include psychotherapy like [[cognitive-behavioral-therapy|Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)]], [[detoxification|detoxification]], a partial hospitalization program (PHP), an intensive outpatient program (IOP), group sessions, and family therapy.
