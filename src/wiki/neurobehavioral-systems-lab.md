# Overview
The University of Kentucky's Neurobehavioral Systems Lab (NSL) focuses on the neural mechanisms of decision-making and the cognitive impairments that result from [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]], particularly resulting from [[cocaine|cocaine]], [[opioid|opioids]], and [[cannabis|cannabis]]. For instance, they use [[neuroimaging|neuroimaging]] techniques to model brain activity computationally and examine how brain circuits are affected by chronic substance use or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS).
