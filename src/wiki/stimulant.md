# Overview
Stimulants are a class of psychoactive substances that speed up activity in the central nervous system (CNS).

Commercially available stimulants include:

- Caffeine

- Nicotine

Prescription stimulants include:

- Adderall

- Ritalin (Methylphenidate)

- Concerta

- Dexamphetamines

Illicit stimulants used recreationally include:

- Cocaine

- Methamphetamine

- Synthetic [[cathinones|cathinones]] ([[bath-salts|bath salts]])
