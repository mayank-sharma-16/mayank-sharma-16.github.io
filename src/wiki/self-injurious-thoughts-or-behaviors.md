# Overview
Self-injurious thoughts and behaviors (SITBs) refer to any thoughts and behaviors that involve the intent to harm oneself, with suicidal intent being possible but not required. 

Suicidal ideation refers specifically to thoughts about ending one's life, and it can be either passive (wishing to go to sleep and not wake up) or active (making specific plans that include a method and date).

Intentional damage without suicidal intent is called non-suicidal self-injury (NSSI), which commonly includes cutting, scratching, burning, hitting, or banging oneself. It's considered a maladaptive way to cope with overwhelming negative emotions.
