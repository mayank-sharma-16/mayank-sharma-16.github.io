# Overview
The Recovery Research Institute is a nonprofit research institute of Massachusetts General Hospital, which is affiliated with Harvard Medical School. Their projects include:

- The Recovery Bulletin, a free monthly publication that summarizes global research in addiction science to over 20,000 professionals and policymakers

- [[https://www.recoveryanswers.org/addiction-ary/#addiction-tourism|The Addictionary]], a glossary that describes a unified scientific language

- National Recovery Study, providing data on 22 million Americans who have had some definition of success in recovery from [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]

The institute's data has been cited by the [[surgeon-general|U.S. Surgeon General]].
