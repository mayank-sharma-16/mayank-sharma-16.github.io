# Overview 
Novel psychoactive substances (also called new psychoactive substances) refer to synthetic compounds that are intended to mimic the effects of other substances while dodging existing regulations. The term is often used interchangeably with [[designer-drugs|designer drugs]]. 

The main examples include:

- Synthetic [[cannabinoids|cannabinoids]]

- Synthetic [[cathinones|cathinones]], also called [[bath-salts|bath salts]]

- Synthetic opioids like [[nitazenes|nitazenes]]

- Designer [[benzodiazepines|benzodiazepines]]

- Synthetic [[hallucinogenic|hallucinogens]]
