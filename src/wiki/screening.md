# Overview
A screening is a brief structured process to identify risk factors or progression of a condition and its symptoms. In clinical psychology, these can consist of short questionnaires or self-report forms that warrant follow-up assessments that are more thorough. Common screenings in mental health treatment include:

- Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9): evaluates the symptom severity of depression

- Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7 (GAD-7): evaluates chronic stress and anxiety

- PCL-5: evaluates the symptoms of Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

- AUDIT/DAST-10: evaluates the risks around alcohol and substance use
