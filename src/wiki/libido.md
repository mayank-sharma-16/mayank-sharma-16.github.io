# Overview
Libido has several meanings in different contexts, but it generally refers to one's desires for sex. Some schools of psychology and philosophy use the word to mean the general desire or energy of life which is represented best by the example of reproductive instincts and patterns. 

If we analyze libido through a [[biopsychosocial-models|biopsychosocial]] perspective, we can neatly delineate some of the factors that impact one's libido:

- Biological: hormones are the primary biological determinant behind one's libido, and drugs (pharmaceutical and otherwise) can influence hormones to either increase or decrease libido

- Psychological: stress, moods, and self-image play roles in libido, obviously; the human body tends to deprioritize reproductive functions when under sustained stress, although some people react to stress by using sex as an escape or dopamine distraction.

- Social: the intimacy, comfort, and communication between partners, especially in long-term relationships, impacts libido as well
