# Overview
"Breathwork" refers to breathing techniques that can help reduce stress and [[anxiety|anxiety]], decrease blood pressure and heart rate, strengthen respiratory muscles, and facilitate emotional processing. 

Most breathwork discussed in a recovery context is about activating the parasympathetic nervous system and soothing the vagus nerve in order to lower stress, reduce anxiety, improve focus, and prepare for sleep:

- 4-7-8 Breathing: inhale through the nose for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, exhale through the mouth for 8 seconds

- Cyclic Sighing: take one deep inhale through the nose, then take one sharp inhale, then release a long slow exhale through the mouth

- Box Breathing: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, hold empty for 4 seconds

- Resonant Breathing: breathe 5 seconds in and 5 seconds out

- Alternate Nostril Breathing (Nadi Shodhana): using the thumb and ring finger, close one nostril, inhale through the open nostril, alternate the nostril that's closed, and exhale through the newly open nostril.  

- Diaphragmatic Breathing (Belly Breathing): place one hand on your chest and one on your belly, and breathe only so that the belly rises and falls

- Humming Bee Breath (Bhramari): inhale deeply, close your ears and eyes with your fingers, and make a long drawn-out humming sound as you exhale
