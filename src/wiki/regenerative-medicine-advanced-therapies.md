# Overview
Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapies (RMAT) are medical treatments that replace or repair damage in the human body that is the underlying cause of disease or injury.

For treating [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]], no RMAT therapy has been approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA), but there are experimental treatments that have progressed to human clinical trials. These include:

-The ALAUNUS Study: Allogeneic Human Mesenchymal Stem Cell (MSC) Infusions for [[comorbidities|comorbid]] [[alcohol-use-disorder|Alcohol Use Disorder (AUD)]] and [[major-depressive-disorder|Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)]] are currently in a [[randomized-controlled-trial|randomized clinical trial]].
