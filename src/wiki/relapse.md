# Overview
A relapse is the return of a condition after a period of its absence or remission. In the context of substance use, it normally means using substances actively again after a period of sobriety or reduction in use. 

In recovery circles, relapse is often considered a gradual process that involves emotional, mental, and physical dimensions. Some failure in routine or external stressor might trigger emotions that lead to mentally rationalizing or glamorizing using a substance again which leads to physically consuming substances again.
