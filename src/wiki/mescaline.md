# Overview
Mescaline is a psychedelic found most often in the peyote cactus, and it can be consumed in various ways orally like teas or capsules. Although it's classified as a Schedule 1 controlled substance in the United States, members of federally-recognized Native American tribes can use and transport peyote for ceremonial purposes. 

The short-term effects of using mescaline include:

- Nausea, vomiting, and loss of appetite

- Heavy sweating and chills

- Elevated blood pressure and heart rate

- Difficulty with motor coordination, including walking

- Anxiety, panic attacks, and paranoia

- Impaired decision-making, mood swings, and disorganized thinking

- Insomnia, fatigue, and body acahes

The long-term effects include:

- Psychological dependence

- Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder (HPDD)

- Exacerbation of Other Conditions, like triggering psychotic reactions for those with conditions like schizophrenia or bipolar disorder
