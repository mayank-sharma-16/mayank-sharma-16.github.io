# Overview
Suppositories are medications that are inserted into a bodily orifice to be melted or dissolved.

Common examples include:

- Osmotic laxatives: stool softeners

- Stimulant laxatives: trigger bowel movements

- Anti-Inflammatory (IBD): address inflammation caused by ulcers or other conditions

- Acetaminophen: pain and fever reducers for those who can't swallow or hold down oral medications

- Preparation H: hemorrhoid treatments that shrink swollen tissues, soothes itching, and reduces inflammation

- Antiemetics: control nausea and vomiting when oral medications can't be held down

- Antifungals: to treat vaginal yeast infections

- Hormone therapies

- pH balancing
