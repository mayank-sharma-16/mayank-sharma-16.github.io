# Overview
The term "psychiatry" was coined by Johann Christian Reil in 1808 to describe a medical approach to addressing mental disorders that was distinct from the field of psychology, which at the time separated the mind from the body as two different systems. Today, psychiatrists are medical doctors with MDs or DOs who address mental, emotional, and behavioral disorders. Although the profession is most commonly associated with [[psychopharmacology|psychopharmacology]], the field includes psychotherapy, [[electroconvulsive-therapy|electroconvulsive therapy]], and lifestyle interventions. They generally follow the DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 for diagnostic standards.
