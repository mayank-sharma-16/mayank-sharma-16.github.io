# Overview
Methadone is a full [[opioid|opioid]] [[agonist|agonist]] used to stop cravings and stabilize brain chemistry without producing any feelings of euphoria in those who struggle with [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]]. It's dispensed through [[opioid-treatment-programs|Opioid Treatment Programs (OTPs)]] which generally require in-person clinic attendance everyday when starting out, which makes it more restricted than other drugs used for [[medication-for-opioid-use-disorder|Medication for Opioid Use Disorder (MOUD)]] like [[buprenorphine|buprenorphine]] that can be prescribed by any physician to be taken home.
