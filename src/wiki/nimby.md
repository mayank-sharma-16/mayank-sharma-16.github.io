# Overview
NIMBY (Not in My Backyard) refers to the various movements opposed to new development within their neighborhoods, generally out of fear that it would impact property values to have services or properties available to low-income people near them. This includes opposition to treatment centers, methadone clinics, syringe service programs, overdose prevention centers, harm reduction facilities, and sober living homes, among other resources for those struggling with substance use. Studies have generally shown that the presence of these services does not increase crime rates in the local neighborhoods.
