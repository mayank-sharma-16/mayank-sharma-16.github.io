# Overview
The Alcohol Research Center (ARC) at the University of Connecticut's health campus is both a research and a clinical institution that conducts trials and studies. Its programs include [[cognitive-behavioral-therapy|Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy]], Individualized Assessment and Treatment Programs (IATP), [[contingency-management|Contingency Management (CM)]], case management, Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT), and routine clinical procedures. 

The ARC is also known for developing screening tools for [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]: Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test (AUDIT) and Alcohol, Smoking and Substance Involvement Screening Test (ASSIST).
