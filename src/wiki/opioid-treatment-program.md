# Overview
Opioid Treatment Programs (OTPs) are licensed medical facilities that provide Medications for Opioid Use Disorder (MOUD), usually with other entry points into health services as well. In the United States, OTPs can dispense methadone, buprenorphine, and naltrexone to eliminate withdrawal symptoms and block cravings when someone is coming off of other opioids. They also generally provide individual therapy, group therapy, counseling services, and peer support services for behavioral health interventions. They might also include toxicology tests, screening for infectious diseases, and distribute naloxone.
