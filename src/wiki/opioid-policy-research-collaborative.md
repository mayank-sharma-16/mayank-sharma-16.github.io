# Overview
The Opioid Policy Research Collaborative (OPRC) was founded in 2017 under the Institute for Behavioral Health (IBH) at Brandeis University leads data-driven approaches to making public policy and public health recommendations for the [[opioid-crisis|opioid-crisis]] and other substance use interventions. Their projects include:

- Massachusetts Drug Supply Data Stream (MADDS): a community drug testing program that allows researchers to issue public health alerts for dangerous additives

- StreetCheck App: a mobile application to standardize sample collection for drug-checking and share results with other groups in a community

- Rapid Assessment of Consumer Knowledge (RACK): ethnographic studies that evaluate substance use patterns and barriers to [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] services

- Respond to Prevent (R2P): a trial investigating how to support pharmacists on educating individuals on using [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]]

- Pharmacy-Based Methadone Toolkit: a resource helping pharmacies work through regulatory barriers so they can begin to dispense [[methadone|methadone]] for [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]]
