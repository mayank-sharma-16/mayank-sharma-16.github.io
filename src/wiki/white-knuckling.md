# Overview
White-knuckling refers to enduring a stressful or painful situation with willpower alone. In the context of substance use, it refers to starting and maintaining sobriety without support of any kind, including handling withdrawals alone. For substances like [[alcohol|alcohol]] and [[benzodiazepines|benzodiazepines]], withdrawals can be potentially fatal. It also typically means foregoing detoxification, tapering, therapy, peer support groups like Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) or Recovery Dharma (RD), medication, and rehabilitation centers.

The term comes from gripping something so tightly that one's knuckles turn white, which represents the result of relying on withstanding tension through willpower alone instead of seeking relief.
