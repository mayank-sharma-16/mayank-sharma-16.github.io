# Overview
The New York Peer AIDS Education Coalition (NYPAEC) was an organization that pushed for [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]] policies from 1989 to 2000. Their operations engaged over 20,000 at-risk clients every year through AIDS educator trainings, [[syringe-services-program|syringe exchanges]], bleach kits, condoms, etc.
