# Overview
Smart Approaches to Marijuana (SAM) is a non-profit that campaigns for the legalization and commercialization of cannabis. It was founded by Patrick J. Kennedy and Kevin Sabet as a nonpartisan "third-way" option between the [[mass-incarceration]] of the disastrous [[war-on-drugs|War on Drugs]] and making weed available [[over-the-counter|over-the-counter (OTC)]] by corporate giants. A core motto guiding their mission is "preventing another [[big-tobacco|big tobacco]]," which is a reference to how much strongly [[tobacco|tobacco]] companies have [[lobbying|lobbied]] governments around the world for favorable policies.
