# Overview
The McShin Foundation is a non-proft Recovery Community Organization (RCO) operating as a [[peer-recovery|peer-driven]] organization for people and families impacted by Substance Use Disorders (SUDs). They offer residential recovery programs, [[sober-living-environment|sober living beds]], partnerships with jails for peer programming, outpatient therapy, suboxone-assisted medication treatments, [[twelve-step|12-step]] support groups, family education, [[yoga|yoga]], and acupuncture.
