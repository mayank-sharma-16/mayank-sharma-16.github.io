# Overview
Spiritual But Not Religious (SBNR) is a term used by those who engage in personal practices and hold sincere beliefs that relate to something deeper or more meaningful than everyday life without following an organized religion or religious sect. About 22% of adults in the United States identify as SBNR. Most who identify as SBNR still believe in God, a universal consciousness, or a spiritual interconnectedness between all things. Many will adopt ideas or concepts from many different religious or spiritual frameworks, and much of the modern landscape is shaped by the influence and legacy of New Age movements.
