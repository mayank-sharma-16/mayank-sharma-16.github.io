# Overview
"Big Tobacco" is a term for the largest companies from the [[tobacco|tobacco]] industry: Philip Morris International (PMI), British American Tobacco (BAT), Japan Tobacco International (JTI), Imperial Brands, China Tobacco, and Altria Group.

Big Tobacco is known for funding a variety of groups that sound harmless or benevolent at a superficial level, like the [[global-action-to-end-smoking|Global Action to End Smoking (GAES)]] organization funded by PMI who neither the World Health Organization nor major universities will work with because of their conflicts of interest. Despite sounding like the group wants to end smoking, they actually promote alternatives to traditional [[cigarettes|cigarettes]], like [[e-cigarettes|e-cigarettes]] which the company also happens to sell. They argue that doing so is [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]], which is arguably true, but it's still motivated by selling addictive substances for profit.
