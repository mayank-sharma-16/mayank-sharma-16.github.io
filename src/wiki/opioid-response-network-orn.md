# Overview
The Opioid Response Network is a coalition of over 40 organizations and 1000 professionals that address [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]], with an emphasis on [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]], at every level from individuals to state governments. It's led by the American Academy of Addiction Psychiatry (AAAP) and funded by the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA).
