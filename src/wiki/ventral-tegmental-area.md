# Overview
The ventral tegmental area (VTA) is a group of neurons in the midbrain where the brain's [[dopamine|dopamine]] pathways originate. Studies show that about 60% of the neurons are dopaminergic, 35% are GABAergic, and 5% are glutamatergic. The [[mesolimbic-system|mesolimbic pathway]] signals from the VTA to the nucleus accumbens and limbic structures for the experiences of reward and pleasure. The mesocortical pathways signals to the prefrontal cortex and are involved in cognition, motivation, and executive function.
