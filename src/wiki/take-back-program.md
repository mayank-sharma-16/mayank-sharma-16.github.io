# Overview
A take-back program allows people to return unwanted goods for proper disposal, including prescription medications, [[sharps|sharps]], electronics, batteries, and hazardous waste. Providing a safe way to dispose of syringes or controlled substances means they're less likely to be end up as litter in public areas and expose others. Flushing medication can actually contaminate ecosystems. [[syringe-services-programs|Syringe Services Programs (SSPs)]] are an example of a take-back program operating under a harm reduction philosophy, as they generally provide clean syringes in exchange for used ones to reduce the incidence of disease transmission.
