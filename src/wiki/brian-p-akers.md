# Overview
Brian Akers is an ethnomycologist who studies [[magic-mushrooms|psychoactive mushrooms]] and their depiction in prehistoric art, like the [[selva-pascuala-mural|Selva Pascuala mural]] in Spain and the [[tassili-mushroom-figures|Tassili mushroom figures]] in Algeria.
