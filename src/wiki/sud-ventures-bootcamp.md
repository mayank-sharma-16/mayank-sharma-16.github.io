# Overview
The MIT SUD Ventures Program is an entrepreneurship program offered through MIT Bootcamps that provides training in building ventures targeting [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]. Their innovation topics have included continuous [[opioid|opioid]] monitoring devices, wearable patches to replace daily pills, online platforms for transitional and [[sober-living-environment|sober housing]], and case studies in existing medical startups.
