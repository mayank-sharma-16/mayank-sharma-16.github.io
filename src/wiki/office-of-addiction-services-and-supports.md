# Overview
The New York State Office of Addiction Services and Supports (OASAS) is the state agency responsible for New York's addiction prevention, [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]], treatment, and [[recovery|recovery]] services. They run:

- 12 Addiction Treatment Centers (ATCs) that serve up to 8000 New Yorkers annually

- oversight of licensing for 1700 community-based programs and 900 treatment facilities that serve over 680,000 individuals a year

- credentialing processes for professionals in the state, such as Certified Alcoholism and Substance Abuse Counselors (CASAC)
