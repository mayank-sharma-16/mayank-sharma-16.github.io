# Overview
HEAL Ohio is an organization that builds networks between mental health and substance use initiatives in the state that advocate for [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]], education, and improved care. Their projects include distributing [[naloxone|naloxone (Narcan)]] through Project DAWN, providing [[syringe-services-program|syringe service programs (SSPs)]], and maintaining harm reduction vending machines that supply test strips for [[fentanyl|fentanyl]].
