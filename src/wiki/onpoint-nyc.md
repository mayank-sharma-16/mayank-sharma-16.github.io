# Articles
OnPoint NYC operates one of the first [[overdose-prevention-centers|Overdose Prevention Centers (OPCs)]] in the United States. Over four years of data tracking, they've saved almost 2000 lives wthout a statistically significant change in drug-related arrests in surrounding neighborhoods, diverting 50,000 instances of illicit substance use away from public areas and into supervised environments. That also has the benefit of keeping millions of units of hazardous public waste and trash, like used syringes, off the streets.
