# Overview
"Maladaptive" means that some pattern of behavior or feelings is poorly aligned with the circumstances surrounding it, leading to undesirable outcomes. 

Increasingly, there has been a trend of describing behaviors as "adapative" or "maladaptive" rather than "healthy" or "unhealthy" to emphasize the importance of context in categorizing behaviors. For example, suppressing emotions may have at one point been an adaptive behavior in response to an environment that punished someone for expressing themselves. Once someone is able to leave that environment, suppressing emotions can be an obstacle to social connectedness and clear communication in a new environment that values emotional vulnerability. This specific example is a type of [[maladaptive-overcontrol|maladaptive overcontrol.]]
