# Overview
Methanol, or methyl alcohol, is a colorless and flammable liquid used to produce [[formaldehyde|formaldehyde]], acetic acid, and other chemicals for plastics and synthetic compounds. It has also been explored as an alternative fuel source that burns clean. Unlike [[ethanol|ethanol]], or drinking alcohol, methanol is incredibly toxic and can cause permanent blindness, comas, and death. Even skin absorption of small amounts can cause severe damage to the body. A very small level of methanol exists in the human body and fermented beverages, and those with a history of chronic drinking will have higher levels. 

Some individuals in active addiction with alcohol might try to use methanol as a substitute for ethanol if they're feeling extremely desperate, which will initially produce a state of drunkenness similar to ethanol in its depression of the central nervous system. However, methanol is [[metabolized|metabolism]] into formaldehyde and then metabolized again into formicacid, which can cause organ failure and death in small amounts.
