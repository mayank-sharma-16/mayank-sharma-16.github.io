# Overview
The Connecticut Community for Addiction Recovery (CCAR) is a centralized resource for addressing [[substance-use-disorder|substance use disorders]] and is formed as a non-profit Recovery Community Organization (RCO). It has financial backing and oversight from the Connecticut Department of Mental Health and Addiction Services (DMHAS). They support physical Recovery Community Centers (RCCs), deploy [[recovery|recovery]] coaches to emergency rooms in hospitals, appoint coaches to prison and parole offices, provide statewide professional training, and operate community-based [[peer-recovery|peer recovery]] coaching for youth ages 12-17 with mental health conditions.
