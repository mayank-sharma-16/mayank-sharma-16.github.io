# Overview
The orbitofrontal cortex (OFC) is a region of the prefrontal cortex (PFC) that's involved in assigning subjective value to choices, evaluating rewards, adapting when rules or rewards change, processing empathy, and regulating moods. In active addiction, the OFC assigns an inflated value to the substance that puts it above survival needs and fails to unlearn the habit of using substances even after it begins to cause harm. After sustained periods of sobriety, the OFC can slowly recovery gray matter volume and begin to reward one for the activities of daily life again.
