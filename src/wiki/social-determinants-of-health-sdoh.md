# Overview
Social determinants of health (SDOH) are the environmental factors that affect health outcomes and quality of life. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) organizes five domains for these factors:

- Economic Stability: employment, income, housing stability, affording healthcare 

- Education Access and Quality: early childhood education, high school graduation, higher education

- Healthcare Access and Quality: nearby health services, health insurance, access to culturally-informed care

- Neighborhood and Built Environment: access to safe hosuing, clean air and water, walkability, crime rates, transportation

- Social and Community Context: support from community organizations, discrimination, exposure to violence

Studies have argued that medical care accounts for only 20% of a person's overall health outcomes while environmental and social factors drive the majority of our health status.
