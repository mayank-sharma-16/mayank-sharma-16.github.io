# Overview
Resentment is the emotion associated with feeling mistreated or subject to injustice that cannot or was not addressed. 

In recovery circles of all types, resentment is considered one of the most common triggers or signs of a relapse that can exist. The idea behind reciting the Serenity Prayer in Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) or embracing impermanence in Recovery Dharma (RD) or Refuge Recovery (RR) is to let go of attachments to events in life that produce resentment. There's also an element of radical acceptance there about accepting that life is going to be unfair and one will suffer for it, and all one can control is the reaction to it. Carrying resentment, in and of itself, only adds distress and triggers to one's life rather than creating a peaceful state of mind.
