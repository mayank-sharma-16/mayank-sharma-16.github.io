# Overview
Residential treatment refers to a live-in program at a specialized facility for mental health and substance use disorders. Usually, it means 24/7 supervision, individual and group therapy, and medication management across 30 to 90 days for longer-term recovery. It is considered a Level 3 operational tier according to the American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM), which is a step down from inpatient hospitalization and a step up from partial hospitalization programs (PHPs). An increasing number of programs describe themselves as dual diagnosis facilities, meaning they address other mental health disorders alongside substance use disorders, like depression, [[anxiety|anxiety]], eating disorders, and PTSD.
