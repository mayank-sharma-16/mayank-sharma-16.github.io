# Overview
Poppers are inhalants made from alkyl nitrites, which produce a minutes-long euphoric sensation when consumed. They're associated with relaxing muscles and widening blood vessels, so they're often associated with party and casual sex scenes. They're commercially available under labels like leather cleaners or nail polish removers.

The side effects of poppers includes:

- [[methemoglobinema|Methemoglobinemia]]: a reduction in oxygen delivery throughout the body which can be potentially fatal

- Vision and eye damage

- Severe headaches

- Fainting
