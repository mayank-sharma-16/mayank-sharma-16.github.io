# Overview
Referring to items that can be purchased at any store by taking it to the counter rather than requiring a prescription or special approval process.

# Historical Context

Over the years, some truly crazy things have been available OTC:

- Benzedrine inhalers: amphetamines as a spray made by Smith, Klein & French (SKF)

- Mrs. Winslow's Soothing Syrup: contained [[morphine|morphine]] and [[alcohol|alcohol]] and was marketed to mothers for use to quiet babies 

- Vin Mariani: a wine infused with [[cocaine|cocaine]] that was endorsed by Pope Leo XIII, Queen Victoria, and Thomas Edison

- Cocaine toothache drops: sold for children to numb dental pain in a small dropper

- Asthma cigarettes: a combination of jimsonweed and belladonna that dilated airways while causing severe hallucinations and delirium

- [[quaaludes|Quaaludes]]

- Radithor: water laced with lethal doses of radioactive Radium isotopes and marketed as "A Cure for the Living Dead"

- Dr. Rose's Arsenic Complexion Wafers: edible wafers loaded with arsenic that claimed to create cleaner, paler skin

- Tapeworm diet pills
