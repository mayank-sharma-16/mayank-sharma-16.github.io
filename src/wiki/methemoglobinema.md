# Overview
Methemoglobinema is a blood disorder in which the iron in hemoglobin becomes oxidized and unable to properly deliver oxygen to boy issues. The most common form is acquired through ingestion of certain chemicals, like benzocaine, dapsone, and nitrites ("poppers"). Sometimes other substances are contaminated with enough of these other chemicals to still trigger the disorder. Less commonly, it can be caused by a genetic deficiency that may cause development delays in infants.
