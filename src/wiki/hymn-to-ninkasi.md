# Overview
The Hymn to Ninkasi is a prayer song that was sung by Sumerian farmers to the goddess of beer [[ninkasi|Ninkasi]], and it is also considered the oldest beer recipe in the world for which evidence currently exists. The hymn was found preserved on cuneiform tablets from the Old Babylonian period, though it functioned as a mnemonic guide for brewers to remember and share the steps of [[fermentation|fermentation]] without written instructions. It also recommends the use of straws since early Sumerian beers were unfiltered, so they contained yeast sediment and bread crumbs that reed or metal straws would filter out through strainers. Examples of this can be seen in works like the [[cylinder-seal-of-queen-puabi]], in which a royal banquet shows a Sumerian queen serving drinks to guests. Also, significantly, brewing and managing taverns that served liquor was conventionally a role women played.
