title: Narcogenocide
----

# Overview
The term "narcogenocide" sees use mostly in historical and academic debates about drug policies being used to suppress and control mass populations.

One of the earliest uses of the term came from methadone clinics funded by the United States federal government in the early 1970s to deal with heroin addiction in urban communities. Many activists from civil rights organizations, including the Black Panther Party, argued that the clinics were a form of "narco-genocide" that ignored the roots of addiction while normalizing the chemical pacification of communities of color through synthetic opioids controlled by the government.
