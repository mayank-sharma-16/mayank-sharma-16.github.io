# Overview
The Kahlert Institute for Addiction Medicine at the University of Maryland was founded in 2023. Their programs include:

- Maryland Addiction Consultation Service (MACS): provides[[training-and-technical-assistance|training and technical assistance (TTA)]] to healthcare teams and pharmacists across the state to manage [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]] and chronic pain

- Research Initiative in Infectious Disease and Substance Use (RIIS): provides care and conducts research into infectious diseases and substance use among disadvantaged and underserved populations

- Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT): provides [[buprenorphine|buprenorphine]] and [[methadone|methadone]] as [[harm-reduction]] methods for [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]]

- [[peer-recovery|Peer counselors]]: provides support for relapse prevention and treatment retention

- Community Advisory Board (CAB): convenes individuals experiencing or recovering from SUD, their family members, and local harm-reduction advocates to address barriers to care like [[stigma|stigma]]

- Comorbidity Research: examining the co-occurrence of SUD with other disorders, like [[anxiety|anxiety]] and [[depression|depression]]
