# Overview
Psychoanalytic therapy brings unconscious thoughts and feelings into conscious awareness for examination and integration, usually relying on Freudian ideas of id, ego, and superego. 

In classic psychoanalysis, a client will free associate their thoughts out loud and the therapist listens for unconscious patterns in their transference. Once the therapist brings them to the client's attention, they are able to process the repressed emotions that were causing them to compulsively repeat an unhealthy pattern. A premise behind this approach is that gaining more insight will help a client exercise their conscious mind to gain control over their unconscious patterns.
