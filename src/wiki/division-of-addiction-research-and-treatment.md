# Overview
The Division of Addiction Research and Treatment (DART) of the University of Maryland School of Medicine is a clinical and research program with several specialized offerings:

- Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT): using medications like [[buprenorphine|buprenorphine]] and [[methadone|methadone]] for [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]]

- Intensive-Outpatient Program (IOP)

- Pratt Street Clinic

- Advanced Depression Treatment Center (ADEPT): specialized care for treatment-resistant [[depression|depression]]

- Telemental Health Program

- Early Intervention Program
