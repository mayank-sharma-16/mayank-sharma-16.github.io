# Overview
Psychotropics are substances that alter the central nervous system's perception, mood, consciousness, and behavior. These include:

- [[antidepressants|Antidepressants]]: these treat depression and anxiety by adjusting the levels of neurotransmitters like serotonin and norepinephrine in the brain. Ex: SSRIs, SNRIs.

- Antipsychotics: these manage the symptoms of [[psychosis|psychosis]], schizophrenia, and bipolar disorder

- Anxiolytics (anti-[[anxiety|anxiety]]): these provide relief for panic, [[phobia|phobia]], and anxiety. Ex: [[benzodiazepines|Benzodiazepines]]

- [[mood-stabilizer|Mood stabilizers]]: these balance extreme mood swings associated with bipolar disorder. Ex: Lithium

- [[stimulant|Stimulants]]: these increase dopamine and norepinephrine activity
