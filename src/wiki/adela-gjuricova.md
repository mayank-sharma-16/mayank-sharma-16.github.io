# Overview
Adela Gjuricova is a historian and researcher whose scholarship focuses on socialist politics and cultures under communism in the context of late 20th century Central Europe, including psychiatric institutions and psychological treatment of [[alcohol-use-disorder|alcohol use disorder (AUD)]] (often called [[alcohol|alcohol]] [[addiction|addiction]] in her works). Her work investigates [[jaroslav-skala|Jaroslav Skala]], a Czech psychiatrist whose approach to addiction treatment included [[detoxification|detoxification]] units and group therapy models that include families. Her exploration of the intersection of treatment for [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]] and socialist ideals involves the design of therapeutic practices across an individualist-collectivist spectrum.
