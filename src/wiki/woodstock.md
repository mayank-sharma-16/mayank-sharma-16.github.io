# Overview
"Woodstock" refers to the Woodstock Music and Art Fair held from August 15 to 18, 1969 in Bethel, New York. The event attracted up to an estimated 500,000 people and became cemented in the history of 1960s counterculture. It was known for having a culture of open air substance use among both the attendees and the performers. The fair's medical director William Abruzzi adopted some early [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] ideas to manage those having bad trips from [[psychedelics|psychedelics]], like "freakout tents" to safely experience volunteers comfort and guide them through hallucinations.
