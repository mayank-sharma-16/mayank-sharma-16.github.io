# Overview
The Addiction Prevention Coalition (APC) is a non-profit dedicated to addressing substance use in the Birmingham area of Central Alabama. Their programs include classroom education, youth leadership initiatives, webinars for families, family support groups, [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]] tools, [[overdose|overdose]] prevention emergency kits, [[peer-recovery|peer recovery specialists]], and historically the End Addiction Bham Walk in downtown Birmingham.
