# Overview 
Screening and Brief Intervention (SBI) is an abridged version of the Screening, Brief Intervention, and Referral to Treatment (SBIRT) framework that identifies problematic substance use patterns. SBI focuses on individuals with mild-to-moderate risk of developing chronic substance use disorders and can make meaningful improvements from simple conversations, usually with motivational interviewing techniques rather than referral to specialized treatment. For facilities or programs that lack the resources for warm-handoffs to specialized treatment, SBI is a viable alternative to settle for compared to SBIRT to still assist early detection and [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] efforts.
