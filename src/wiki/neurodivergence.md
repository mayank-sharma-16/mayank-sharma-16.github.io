# Overview
Neurodivergence means having a brain whose mental processes and behaviors differ from the norm in one's society and culture. 

Examples include:

- Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)

- [[attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder|Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)]]

- Dyslexia, Dyscalculia, and Dysgraphia

- Sensory Processing Disorder

- Tourette Syndrome

- Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

- Synesthesia
