# Overview
Withdrawal is the process of suddenly stopping or reducing a substance that the body has adapted to over time, which is usually accompanied by many unpleasant symptoms. Withdrawal from alcohol and [[benzodiazepines|benzodiazepines]] are especially at-risk of being fatal. Withdrawal from common recreational substances includes:

- Alcohol: High blood pressure, nausea, vomiting, dehydration, tremors, confusion, sweating, insomnia, seizures, delirium tremens (DTs)

- Opioids: flu-like symptoms, nausea, vomiting, sweating, cravings

- Stimulants: exhaustion, sleep disturbances, depressive episodes

- Nicotine: Irritability, headaches, brain fog, restlessness
