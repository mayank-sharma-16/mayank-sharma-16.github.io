# Overview
The Tassili mushroom figures are prehistoric cave paintings discovered at the Tassili n'Ajjer mountain range in Algeria in the early 20th century, with some scholars claiming that it may be the oldest depictions of mushrooms in ritual [[shamanism|shamanism]] in the world with a creation between 7000 - 5000 BCE. 

[[brain-p-akers|Brian P. Akers]] has argued that certain ancient rock paintings depict shamanistic rituals, but he also pointed out that there are unsettled questions around the Tassili figures in terms of their dating and interpretations. They were popularized through [[terence-kemp-mckenna|Terence McKenna's]] work that featured an artistic interpretation of one figure as a "bee-faced mushroom shaman," which was adopted in 1990s [[psychedelic|psychedelic]] cultures.
