# Overview
The Recovery Advocacy Project (RAP) is a national non-profit that gives grassroots organizing tools to people in [[recovery|recovery]] and their families, based around the idea that those in recovery should have a direct voice in political decisions and legislation that addresses their mental health condition. They intentionally court bipartisan support for [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] tools like [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] or expanding funding for [[sober-living-environment|sober living environments (SLEs)]] and transitional housing.
