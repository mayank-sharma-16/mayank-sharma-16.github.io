# Overview
The [[national-harm-reduction-coalition|National Harm Reduction Coalition (NHRC)]] administers the Drug Overdose Prevention and Education (DOPE) Project, which trains bystanders and community groups in San Francisco to recognize the signs of an [[opioid|opioid]] [[overdose|overdose]] and use [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]]. They claim to be the largest citywide naloxone distribution program in the country with over 13,000 people educated on reversing an opioid overdose.
