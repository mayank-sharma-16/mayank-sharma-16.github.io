# Overview
The Stop the Addiction Fatality Epidemic (SAFE) Project is a national non-profit founded in 2017 to support community programs that address substance use, including education, destigmatization, expanding access to treatment and [[recovery|recovery]] resources, and distributing [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] tools like [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] to reverse [[overdose|overdoses]]. Its programs are tailored for communities like college campuses, workplaces, and military families. It was created by James and Mary Winnefeld to honor their late son who passed away from an accidental opioid overdose.
