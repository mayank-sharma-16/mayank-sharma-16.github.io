# Overview
The International Consortium of Universities for Drug Demand Reduction (ICUDDR) maintains the Directory of Addiction Study Programs (DASP), a global database of 1000 educational institutions around the world that offer degrees related to [[addiction|addiction]] science and [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]].
