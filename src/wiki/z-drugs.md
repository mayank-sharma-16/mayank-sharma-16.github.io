# Overview
Z-drugs are a class of [[sedative-hypnotic|sedative-hypnotic]] medications mainly used for insomnia. These include:

- Zolpidem (Ambien)

- Eszopiclone (Lunesta)

- Zaleplon (Sonata)

- Zopiclone (Zimovane, Immovane)

They act as non-benzodiazepine [[benzodiazepine|benzodiazepine]] receptor [[agonist|agonists]] (NBBRAs) and bind to GABA receptors in the brain, which slows brain activity and causes sleepiness. Although designed to be less [[habit-forming|habit-forming]] than conventional benzodiazepines, they still come with a risk of [[dependence|dependence]] and [[withdrawal|withdrawals]] if used repeatedly or in excess.
