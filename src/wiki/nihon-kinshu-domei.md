# Overview
Nihon Kinshu Domei, or the "Japan Temperance Union" in English, is an organization dedicated to preventing alcohol-related health issues and supporting [[rehab|rehabilitation programs]] for alcoholics.

# Historical Context
Established in 1898 and called Nihon Kinshu Domei-kai ("Japan Temperance League"), the organization was founded as a nationwide consolidation of regional [[temperance|temperance]] groups. 

In 1922, after 22 years of lobbying the National Diet, Nihon Kinshu Domei pushed for the passage of the Law Prohibiting Minors from Drinking to address underage drinking.

In the 1950s, Nihon Kinshu Domei introduced Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) to Japan, which was adapted for Japanese culture through the growth of [[danshukai|Danshukai]] groups.
