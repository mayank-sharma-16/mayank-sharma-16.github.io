# Overview
Sober living environments (SLEs), also known as sober living houses or recovery residences, are shared housing arrangements that enforce substance-free policies while facilitating recovery meetings and treatments. It's common for clients who complete inpatient or [[residential-treatment|residential treatment]] programs to eventually stay in SLEs while completing a partial hospitalization program (PHP), an intensive outpatient program (IOP), or working before having the means to live independently. As a point of clarification, residential treatment centers are considered clinical environments that provide on-site treatment and therapy while SLEs are generally peer-based housing.
