# Overview
"Drug of choice" (DOC) refers to someone's primary preference for a recreational substance. Many of those who struggle with [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]] often struggle with one primary substance the most, even if they use other substances. The most common example is typically someone who says that they use [[cannabis|weed]] or [[magic-mushrooms|shrooms]], but [[alcohol|alcohol]] is what's actively ruining their life or driving the worst of the destruction. For those with a history of [[polysubstance|polysubstance]] use, there may not be a clear DOC to pick, so many will often choose their first substance that served as a gateway into the others.
