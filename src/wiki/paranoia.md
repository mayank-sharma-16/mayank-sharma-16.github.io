# Overview
Paranoia in its casual, everyday sense can refer to an attitude that is suspicious of others and hypervigilant in anticipation of something going wrong. When it's mentioned in medical literature, it refers more specifically to a state in which someone holds rigid beliefs or delusions from reality that can't be changed through evidence or logic. In some clinical presentations, it can come through Paranoid Personality Disorder (PPD), or a chronic a pattern of mistrust and inflexibility that interprets benign actions as malicious without full psychotic breaks. What separates paranoia from anxiety is that paranoia involves assigning some kind of malicious intent to forces that intervene in one's life, rather than simply a worry about negative outcomes.
