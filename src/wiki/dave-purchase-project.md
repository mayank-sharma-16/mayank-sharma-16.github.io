# Overview
The Dave Purchase project ran the first legal [[syringe-services-program|syringe exchange program]] in the country and [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] supply co-op. Alongside running the [[tacoma-needle-exchange|Tacoma Needle Exchange (TNE)]], they also deliver [[Naloxone (Narcan)]], safer smoking tools, alcohol wipes, [[sharps|sharps]] containers, and [[fentanyl|fentanyl]] test strips. One of their programs includes the [[north-american-syringe-exchange-network|North American Syringe Exchange Network (NASEN)]] initiative, which secures low-cost access to harm reduction resources in bulk.
