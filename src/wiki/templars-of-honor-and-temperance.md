# Overview
The Templars of Honor and Temperance was founded in New York in 1845 as a fraternal Christian organization to retain [[abstinence|abstinence]] from alcohol in order to keep the body clean as a temple for the Holy Spirit. It was once called the Marshall Temperance Fraternity, and it was founded by John Murphy, R.T. Traill, and A.D. Wilson out of frustration with the [[sons-of-temperance|Sons of Temperance]] for having rituals that were too plain. When [[prohibitionism|Prohibition]] happened through the 18th Amendment in 1919, [[temperance|temperance]] groups experienced a decline in public interest and new potential members since the urgency to act on alcohol was gone. By 1938, the organization transferred its Supreme Council to Europe because of the collapsing US branches. It was renamed Tempel Riddare Orden and remains active in Nordic countries like Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Iceland, and the Faroe Islands with thousands of members.
