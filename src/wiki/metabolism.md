# Overview
Metabolism refers to the series of reactions that a body undergoes to turn its inputs like food and water into energy to maintain itself. Any intermediate or end product that results from a metabolic process is called a [[metabolite|metabolite.]]

The way drugs are metabolized in the body determines everything from its intensity to duration to side effects, and that depends both on the nature of the drug itself and the unique biology of each living thing. Genetic differences between people changes the rate that substances are metabolized at. While they're in the body, substances add a lot of stress on metabolic processes that can cause long-term damage to organs.
