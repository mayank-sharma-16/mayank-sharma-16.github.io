# Overview
The University of Connecticut Recovery Community (URC) supports students who struggle with mental health issues, including [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]. They arrange [[peer-recovery|peer-led]] services for individual coaching, diverse [[recovery|recovery]] meetings including [[yoga|yoga]], substance-free study and housing spaces, and sober recreational activities.
