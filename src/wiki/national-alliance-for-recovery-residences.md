# Overview
The National Alliance for Recovery Residences (NARR) is a non-profit that sets national standards for recovery housing like [[sober-living-environment|sober living environments (SLEs)]] in the United States. Founded in 2011, they merged with the Association of Halfway House Alcoholism Programs (AHAP) in 2013. NARR operates on an affiliate network model, which means that it accredits regional organizations which manage inspections for operators in their regions. State health departments will often require a sober living home to receive a NARR-compliant certification from a regional affiliate in order to gain access to subsidies and referrals.
