# Overview
Psychedelics are a class of psychoactive substances that act as [[agonist|agonists]] or partial agonists at the brain's serotonin receptors. 

The most common examples include:

- Lysergic acid diethylamide (LSD): a synthetic derivative of ergot fungi 

- [[psilocybin|Psilocybin]]: a natural compound found in [[magic-mushrooms|magic mushrooms]]

- [[mescaline|Mescaline]]: a natural compound derived from cacti like peyote and San Pedro

- [[dimethyltryptamine|Dimethyltryptamine (DMT)]]: a compound found in plants and brewed as [[ayahuasca|ayahuasca]]
