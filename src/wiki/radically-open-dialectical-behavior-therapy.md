# Overview
Radically Open Dialectical Behavior Therapy (RO-DBT) is an approach to disorders of emotional overcontrol (OC) that departs from traditional DBT, which targets dysregulation in the form of undercontrol. This means focusing on excessive self-control, perfectionism, rigidity, risk aversion, low receptivity, inhibited emotional expression, social-signaling deficits, vulnerability, and openness through disconfirming experiences. It can help in cases of [[anorexia-nervosa|anorexia nervosa]], treatment-resistant [[depression|depression]], obsessive-compulsive personality disorder (OCPD), autism spectrum disorders, and other personality disorders involving avoidance and paranoia.
