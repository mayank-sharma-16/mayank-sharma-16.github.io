# Overview
Mood stabilizers are medications that address extreme mood swings, as are seen during manic and depressive episodes in bipolar disorder. The most common mood stabilizers include:

- Lithium

- Anticonvulsants: valproic acid/divalproex, lamotrigine, carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine

- Atypical Antipsychotics: quetiapine, olanzapine, risperidone

They generally work by calming overactive neural pathways in the brain, including GABA and glutamate, and they take weeks of consistent use to reach clinical effectiveness. They also shouldn't be stopped suddenly in case of symptoms from withdrawal or relapse.
