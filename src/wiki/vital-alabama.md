# Overview
VitAL Alabama is a public health initiative based out of the University of Alabama that works to improve mental health and [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] care across the state. Their projects include Connect Alabama as a mobile resource funded by federal CDC grants, suicide prevention screenings in rural counties, and the VitAL Annual Conference as a behavioral health summit. They also host other circuits, like the "Stop Judging, Start Healing" Stigma Summits and the Justice and Substance Use Alabama Summit.
