# Overview
The American Psychological Association's Dictionary of Psychology defines stress as:

*"the physiological or psychological response to internal or external stressors. Stress involves changes affecting nearly every system of the body, influencing how people feel and behave. For example, it may be manifested by palpitations, sweating, dry mouth, shortness of breath, fidgeting, accelerated speech, augmentation of negative emotions (if already being experienced), and longer duration of stress fatigue."*

# Models

## Conservation of Resources (COR) Model
Stevan Hobfoll developed a framework called the Conservation of Resources model that begins with the premise that individuals generally try to gain and preserve resources in a given environment. Stress occurs when resources are threatened, when there's a net loss in resources, or when investments in gaining resources fail to pan out.
