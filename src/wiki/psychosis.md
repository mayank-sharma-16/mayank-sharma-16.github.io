# Overview
Psychosis is a state of mind involved with a detachment from reality through hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thinking, and abnormal motor behavior.

Psychosis and substance use have a bidirectional relationship. Substances like marijuana, cocaine, methamphetamine, alcohol, and others can trigger hallucinations, paranoia, and delusions. Some people who have an underlying mental health disorder, or a predisposition for one, might have an episode triggered by substance use. At the same time, those who feel distress as a result of psychosis from another mental disorder may end up using substances to cope with it. 50% of those diagnosed with schizophrenia also report struggling with substance use.
