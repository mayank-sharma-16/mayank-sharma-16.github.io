# Overview
The MIT Community Development and Substance Abuse Center (CDSA) addresses the university's campus cultur around substance use. Their services include:

- [[brief-alcohol-screening-and-intervention|Brief Alcohol Screening and Intervention for College Students (BASICS)]]

- PartySafe Training

- Peer Education Alliances: partnerships with student safety groups to embed [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] practices into campus culture

- Self-Assessment Tools
