# Overview
Safe sex refers to sexual practices that reduce the risk of spreading sexually transmitted diseases (STDs) and having unplanned pregnancies. 

With substance use comes impaired judgment and impulsive decision-making, which means it's more likely someone will fail to take the precautions of ensuring sex is safe. That significantly increases the chances of transmitting diseases like HIV or Hepatitis B and C. For a subset of the population with substance use disorders, sex work and transactional sex are likely to facilitate unsafe sex.
