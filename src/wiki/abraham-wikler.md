# Overview
Abraham Wikler was an American psychiatrist who was among the earliest to study the theory of [[addiction|addiction]] and [[relapse|relapse]] through [[behaviorism|behavioral]] [[conditioning|conditioning]] theories. He observed that drug-seeking behaviors are motivated by both [[positive-reinforcement|positive reinforcement]] through pleasurable effects and [[negative-reinforcement|negative reinforcement]] through the relief of [[withdrawal|withdrawal]] symptoms, which laid the foundation for [[naltrexone|naltrexone]] as an [[antagonist|antagonist]] that blocks the effects of [[opioid|opioids]] and [[alcohol|alcohol]]. He also noted that certain cues from the environment served as [[trigger|triggers]] that induced [[craving|cravings]] or other types of psychological distress.
