# Overview
Valence is the attractiveness of an object or event. Positive valence makes something desirable while negative valence means that something should be avoided. [[valence-allocation|Valence allocation]] describes the process of assigning valence to the stimuli that one experiences. Evolutionary theorists argue that valence is a shortcut that gives organisms a instant gut-level calculation of whether something is beneficial or harmful rather than a slow logical calculation that freezes a system until it's completed.
