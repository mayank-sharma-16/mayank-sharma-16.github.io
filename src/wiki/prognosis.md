# Overview
A prognosis is a prediction about the likely course a health condition will take and the outcomes a patient might have.

With substance use disorders, the general prognosis is that it's a chronic brain disease that involves relapses and remissions with many treatment options, and success is generally predicted based on recovery capital, co-occurring health conditions, severity of use patterns, and number of recovery attempts. Once someone has been able to maintain sobriety for 5 years, the likelihood of remaining sober is over 85%.
