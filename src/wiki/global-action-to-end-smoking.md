# Overview
Global Action to End Smoking (GAES) is a non-profit established in 2017 that claims to accelerate the elimination of [[tobacco|tobacco]]-use around the world. The World Health Organization (WHO) and major universities like Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health refuse to work with this organization, perhaps because it's fully funded by Philip Morris International (PMI), which has historically sold combustible [[cigarettes|cigarettes]].

Organizations like GAES argue for alternatives to traditional smoking like [[e-cigarettes|e-cigarettes]], which their backers at PMI also sell as products. Whether people buy cigarettes or e-cigarettes, there's a chance for the same tobacco and nicotine companies to profit.
