# Overview
Relapse prevention refers to coping skills developed to manage the triggers, risk factors, or warnings signs of a relapse.

# Models

## Marlatt and Gordon Relapse Prevention Model
The Marlatt and Gordon Relapse Prevention Model is a cognitive-behavioral framework developed by the same researchers who noted the [[abstinence-violation-effect|Abstinence Violation Effect (AVE)]].

## The CENAPS Model
The CENAPS Model proposes nine principles to sustain recovery:

1. Stabilization

2. Assessment

3. Relapse Education

4. Warning Sign Identification

5. Warning Sign Management

6. Recovery Planning

7. Inventory Training

8. Family Involvement

9. Follow-Up
