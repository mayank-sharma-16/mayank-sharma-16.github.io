# Overview
Twelve-Step Facilitation (TSF) is a psychotherapy designed to promote abstinence through connection with 12-step fellowships like Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) and Narcotics Anonymous (NA). Unlike [[cognitive-behavioral-therapy|Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)]] which emphasizes self-empowerment through internal autonomy and coping skills, TSF explicitly aims to hand off a client into a fellowship that provides external resources to support a client's internal work through the Steps. TSF builds on AA/NA concepts of a Higher Power as a remedy to personal powerlessness over a substance.
