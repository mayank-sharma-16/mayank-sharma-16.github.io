# Overview
Whole-person care is a [[holistic|holistic]] approach to healthcare that addresses the entire individual with physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual dimensions of their well-being.

The National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) proposed the Whole Person Health Index (WPHI) as a 9-item self-reporting instrument to screen someone's general health.
