# Overview
Opioid Use Disorder (OUD) is a more specific category of Substance Use Disorder (SUD), and there are dedicated [[opioid-treatment-program|Opioid Treatment Programs (OTPs)]] that are licensed to provide therapy, medication, and peer support. Historically, the term [[morphinism|morphinism]] described essentially the same diagnosis.
