# Overview
The Counseling and Mental Health Services (CAMHS) under [[harvard-university-health-services|Harvard University Health Services (HUHS)]] was established in 2018. Their initial services include consultations through Care Coordinators to connect with clinicians that specialize in [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]] and co-occurring disorders like [[anxiety|anxiety]] and [[depression|depression]].
