# Overview
The yage vine has been used by the indigenous Amazonian nations for thousands of years, particularly to make drinks like ayahuasca. Over 70 different groups, including the Tukano, Siona, Inga, and Shipibo, have their own names and preparation styles of the vine. In generall, it was consumed by shamans and healers as part of ritual practices rather than ordinary people as a recreational substance. Studies have shown that the alkaloids in yage stimulate [[neuroplasticity|neuroplasticity]], or the creation of new neural pathways. When brewed by itself instead of a companion plant with DMT, it's not a psychedelic and behaves more like a sedative.
