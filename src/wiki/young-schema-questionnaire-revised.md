# Overview
The Young Schema Questionnaire-Revised (YSQ-R) is a 116-item self-report questionnaire that measures [[early-maladaptive-schemas|Early Maladaptive Schemas (EMS)]]. It was developed in 2022 as a refinement of Jeffrey Young's YSQ-L3 using Rasch analysis.
