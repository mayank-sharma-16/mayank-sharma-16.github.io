# Overview
Ninkasi is the Sumerian goddess of brewing, beer, and alcoholic beverages. The c. 1800 BCE [[hymn-to-ninkasi|Hymn to Ninkasi]] is widely considered the oldest beer recipe in the world, which functioned as both prayer and a mnemonic guide for brewers to remember without a common system of written manuals.
