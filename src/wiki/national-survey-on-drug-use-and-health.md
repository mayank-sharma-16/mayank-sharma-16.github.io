# Overview
The National Survey on Drug Use and Health (NSDUH) is sponsored by the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA), and it annually surveys 70,000 members of the American civilian population aged 12 or older in all 50 states and Washington, D.C. It excludes those who are unhoused, active-duty military, or institutionalized in jails or hospitals.

The percentage of Americans experiencing a substance use disorder (SUD) in the past year dropped from 16.8% in 2024 to 15.3% to 2025. Any Mental Illness (AMI) percentages decreased from 23.4% to 20.6%. Only 1 in 6 of those who qualified for clinical SUD treatment were able to receive it.
