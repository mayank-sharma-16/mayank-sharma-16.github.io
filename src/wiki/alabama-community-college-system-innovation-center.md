# Overview
The Alabama Community College System (ACCS) Innovation Center provides specialized workforce training tailored to high-demand industries in the state of Alabama. Their work on substance use includes partnering with the [[alabama-alliance-for-recovery-residences|Alabama Alliance for Recovery Residences (AARR)]] for Alabama Recovery Advocacy Day, hosting the "Recovery Is Here" Campaign, and workforce training for individuals [[recovery|recovering]] from [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] or transitioning out of correctional facilities. They also provide training and certification for frontline mental and community health workers.
