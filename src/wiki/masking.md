# Overview
"Masking" refers to concealing one's natural traits to avoid being treated differently, which can be either conscious or unconscious. 

Individuals with autism might suppress stimming or mirror body language to fit in. Those with ADHD might suppress hyperactivity or inattentive patterns to be able to conform to standards. Anyone with depression or anxiety who forces a smile all day at work to appear happy is masking too. These are adaptations to the way society expects people to behave, and they can be an incredible drain on mental and physical health. In fact, there's a negative feedback loop: those who mask will also likely try to mask the symptoms of masking. This can interfere with mental health diagnoses and accommodations since the individual is used to concealing symptoms under stressful conditions.
