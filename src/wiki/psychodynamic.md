# Overview
Psychodynamic therapy explores the patterns in someone's internalized scripts in terms of an internal object, an internal self, and their affective link. 

In a classic psychodynamic approach, a client and therapist engage in a collaborative conversation to find the unconscious scripts a client repeats in their daily life that drives unhealthy patterns in their behaviors and relationships. Once the client begins to use a script with the therapist and identifies the past memories associated with it, they can use the therapist to experience a validating relationship that challenges the old script.
