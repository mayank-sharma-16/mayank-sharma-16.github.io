# Overview
A Sequential, Multiple Assignment, Randomized Trial (SMART) is a type of clinical trial used for adaptive interventions that change treatment plans based on how patients respond. This allows researchers to evaluate options for first-line treatment, alternatives for non-responders, and maintenance for responders. For conditions like substance use disorder that involve chronic and complex needs with constant adjustments in levels of care, SMART can be especially helpful.
