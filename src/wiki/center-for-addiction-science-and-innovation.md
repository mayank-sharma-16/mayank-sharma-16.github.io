# Overview
The Center for Addiction Science and Innovation (CASI) was launched in 2026 at the University of Connecticut as an interdisciplinary research initiative. They:

- explore the [[neurobiology|neurobiological]] changes caused by chronic substance use

- discover new drugs that can reverse or block the cravings of [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]

- engineer smart delivery systems like nanoparticles

- investigate the rising [[dependence|dependence]] on [[kratom|kratom]]

- assess early data on the therapeutic value of [[psychedelics|psychedelics]] and [[cannabis|cannabis]]

- host training seminars to help students identify [[overdose|overdoses]] and administer [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]]
