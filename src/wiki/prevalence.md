# Overview
Prevalence refers to how often something happens.

Key metrics about the prevalence of various phenomena around substance use:

- over 58% of people aged 12 and older have used a substance in the past month

- up to 20% of American adults experience substance use disorder 

- alcohol is the most common single substance use disorder

- young adults aged 18-25 have the highest rate of drug use at 39%

- males have a higher prevalence of SUD than females (20% vs 14.3%)
