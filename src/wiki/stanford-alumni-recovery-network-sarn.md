# Overview
The Stanford Alumni Recovery Network (SARN) is a support network of Stanford students, alumni, and affiliates advocating for those with mental health disorders, including [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]]. The group's members maintain strict [[confidentiality|confidentiality]] as a [[peer-recovery|peer recovery resource]] facilitating [[recovery|recovery]]-based living arrangements and sober recreational activities.
