# Overview
Peptides are chains of amino acids that serve as the building blocks of proteins, and there are over 100 peptide medications approved by the FDA. Common examples include:

- insulin: for diabetes

- GLP-1 receptor agonist: for diabetes and weight management, like semaglitude and tirzepatide

- teriparatide: for osteoporosis

In wellness and fitness spaces, there are trends of people taking unregulated peptides that are sold under legal loopholes, like claiming to be "research chemicals:"

- Body Protection Compound-157 (BPC-157): despite the lack of evidence supporting its efficacy and a ban from the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA), it's used with the belief that it speeds up healing in the muscle and tendons

- Thymosin Beta-4 fragment (TB-500): often paired with BPC-157 under similarly unsubstantiated claims about its ability to improve healing and flexibility

- CJC-1295 & Ipamorelin: together they trigger the secretion of more growth hormone and are taken with the goal of lowering body fact and building lean muscle mass

The risk of consuming these unregulated compounds goes beyond just a lack of scientific evidence about how they impact the body; because they're bought off a gray market that bypasses traditional regulations and testing, they can be contaminated with bacteria, heavy metals, other substances, or simply incorrect dosing information.
