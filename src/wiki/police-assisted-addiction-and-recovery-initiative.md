# Overview
The Police Assisted Addiction and Recovery Initiative (PAARI) is a national non-profit that helps law enforcement develop [[diversion|diversionary]] pathways to treatment and recovery without involving arrest. PAARI now has over 800 partners in law enforcement across 46 states. They provide [[training-and-technical-assistance|training and technical assistance (TTA)]], like the 2026 "Essential Deflection and Diversion Certification Program" to standardize diversion practices among law enforcement. 

# Historical Context
PAARI was founded in 2015 in Gloucester, Massachusetts as part of the "Angel Program" run by local law enforcement that allowed anyone to walk into a police station to turn over drugs without fear of arrest and receive placement into a [[recovery|recovery]] program.
