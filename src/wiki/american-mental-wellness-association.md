# Overview
The American Mental Wellness Association (AMWA) is a national non-profit dedicated to raising awareness and supporting programs for mental wellness. One of their key arguments is that substance use is often motivated by the self-medication of disorders that can be screened for and stabilized early. They provide resources for education, prevention, early detection, early intervention, and research initiatives:

- a proprietary questionnaire adapted from the Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Test (MMPI), which flags shifts in behavior or psychological state before they lead to crises

- 8-hour certification courses for evidence-based Mental Health First Aid (MHFA) training

- Girl Scout Patch Program as a specialized curriculum to cultivate emotional intelligence and self-care in young girls

- educational toolkits and children's books, like "Understanding Your Anxiety for Elementary Children"

- rapid response strategy plans, which outline community interventions that address gaps in local mental health care
