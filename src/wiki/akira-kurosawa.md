# Overview
Akira Kurosawa was a Japanese filmmaker who created contemporary dramas in the aftermath of WWII, where [[addiction|addiction]] was a characteristic of postwar trauma and social oppression. Kurosawa was known to be a heavy drinker and chain smoker, but it was his collaborator Toshiro Mifune who was known to struggle with severe [[dependence|dependency]] that impacted his behavior and health. Reportedly, Kurosawa once drank heavily with Andrei Tarkovsky in Moscow after a screening of Solaris.
