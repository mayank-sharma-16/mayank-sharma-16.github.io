# Overview
Salvia is a psychoactive plant from the mint family with hallucinogenic properties through the compound salvinorin A. While [[hallucinogenic|hallucinogens]] like LSD or the [[psilocybin|psilocybin]] found in [[magic-mushrooms|magic mushrooms]] target serotonin, salvinorin A binds to kappa-opioid receptors. It causes intense [[psychedelic|psychedelic]] experiences and dissociations that can last up to an hour depending on whether it's smoked or chewed. In the United States, it is not federally an illicit substance, though some states and other countries have restricted it.
