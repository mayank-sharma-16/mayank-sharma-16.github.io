# Overview
Paregoric is a medication for severe diarrhea that contains morphine derived from [[opium|opium]] that includes [[ethanol|ethanol]], benzoic acid, camphor, and anise oil. It's classified as a Schedule III controlled substance in the United States and is considered [[habit-forming|habit-forming]].

Side effects of paregoric include:

- Gastrointestinal distress: constipation, stomach pain, nausea, vomiting

- Central nervous system (CNS) depression: drowsiness, dizziness, lightheadedness

- Cardiovascular shifts: shallow breathing, slowed heart rate, dizziness 

- Difficulty urinating and increased sweating

- Itching or a rash

Because it's an addictive substance that can lead to increasing levels of dependence and tolerance, the severity of symptoms increases alongside prolonged use.
