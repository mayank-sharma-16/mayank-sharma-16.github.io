# Overview
Neuroimaging refers to the technology and techniques used to picture or map the brain's structures and activity. The most commonly used ones include:

- Computed Tomography (CT)

- Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)

- Functional MRI (fMRI)

- PET/SPECT (Positron Emission Tomography / Single-Photon Emission Computed Tomography)

- Electroencephalography (EEG)

- Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy (MRS)

Advancements in neuroimaging have played a substantial role in providing evidence for the disease model of addiction and encouraging a shift towards treatment rather than punitive incarceration.
