# Overview
Sedative-hypnotics are central nervous system (CNS) [[depressant|depressants]] that relieve anxiety or aid sleep by olwing down brain activity. Sedatives on their own generally just reduce anxiety and excitement, but hypnotics specifically target the start of sleep cycles. Many medications actually act on a spectrum between them, with lower doses having a sedative effect while higher doses produces a hypnotic effect. [[benzodiazepines|Benzodiazepines]], [[barbiturate|barbiturates]], and melatonin agonists are all examples.
