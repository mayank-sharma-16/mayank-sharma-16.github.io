# Overview
The Center for Addiction Recovery Support (CARS) is a federally funded program managed through the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA). They provide training and technical assistance (TTA) to nonprofits that are part of the national [[peer-recovery|peer recovery]] infrastructure. Their projects include integrating peer-support across the justice system, professionalizing the peer recovery workforce, improving data collection for evidence-based approaches, producing toolkits and roadmaps like the "Recovery Friendly Workplace Toolkit," and publishing field evaluations like the "Recovery Community Needs Assessment Report."

They also maintain a national online [[cars-peer-recovery-organizations|Directory of Peer Recovery Organizations]] that tracks support centers, recovery cafes, and clubhouses.
