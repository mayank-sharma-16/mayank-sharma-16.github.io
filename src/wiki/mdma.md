# Overview
3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine (also known as MDMA, ecstacy, X, E, molly) is a psychedelic stimulant known to cause euphoria, heightened sensory perceptions, and deep emotional connections. Its immediate side effects include:

- Bruxism: involuntary clenching of the jaw or teeth grinding

- Hyperthermia: high body temperature

- Cardiovascular stress: elevated heart rate and high blood pressure

- Dehydration

- Hyponatremia: low blood sodium levels caused by drinking too much water too quickly

- Chills, sweats, and nausea

- Dry Mouth

- Blurred Vision

- Anxiety, panic, or paranoia

- Distorted Time Perception

The "come down" from an ecstacy high is known for involving a day or more of severe depression and anxiety. Long-term, chronic use of ecstacy can play a role in the development or symptom severity of:

- Memory deficits

- Attention issues

- Depression

- Sleep disorders

- Chronic anxiety

- Organ damage
