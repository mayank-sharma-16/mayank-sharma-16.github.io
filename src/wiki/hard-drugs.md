# Overview
"Hard drugs" is a term that loosely refers to substances that have powerful effects, a high risk of forming [[dependence|dependence]] or [[habit-forming|habits]], and a higher chance of irreversible or fatal consequences like an [[overdose|overdose]]. Examples of typical hard drugs include [[heroin|heroin]], [[cocaine|cocaine]], [[fentanyl|fentanyl]] and [[methamphetamine]]. 

As a general rule of thumb, if it's not [[alcohol|alcohol]], [[caffeine|caffeine]], [[cannabis|cannabis]], [[over-the-counter|over-the-counter (OTC)]], or a naturally-occurring [[psychoactive|psychoactive]] herb like [[kratom|kratom]] and [[kava|kava]], it can potentially be considered a "hard drug."
