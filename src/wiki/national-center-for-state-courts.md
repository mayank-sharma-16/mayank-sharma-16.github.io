# Overview
The National Center for State Courts (NCSC) is a non-partisan non-profit organization that supports state courts around the United States and around the world. It drives the growth of Problem-Solving Courts, like [[drug-court|Drug Courts]], [[driving-under-influence|DUI]] Courts, and Veterans Treatment Courts, which can use [[diversion|diversion]] through treatment programs instead of traditional sentencing. The NCSC helps courts move through competency restoration backlogs where mental health issues, possibly substance-induced, impaired a defendant's legal competency. It also manages the National Treatment Court Resource Center to track treatment court metrics against health and demographic data.
