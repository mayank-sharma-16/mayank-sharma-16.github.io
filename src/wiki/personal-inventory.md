# Overview
"Personal inventory" is a term popularized by 12-step programs that refers to the practice of examining yourself to build awareness over what you can change. 

## Twelve-Step 
There are two steps that discuss personal inventories. 

Step 4 says: "*Made a searching and fearless moral inventory of ourselves*"

This step is meant to catalog the moral and psychological baggage one has accumulated throughout one's life, generally structured into a split between Resentments, Fears, Sexual Conduct, Harms, etc. This is then shared with a trusted person, commonly a sponsor.

Step 10 says: "*Continued to take personal inventory and when we were wrong promptly admitted it.*"

This step is an aspect of regular maintenance in which one examines their behavior to see what they could have done differently and plans to make amends if possible. It serves to diffuse the Resentments, Fears, Sexual Conduct, and Harms of Step 4 before they become bigger issues.
