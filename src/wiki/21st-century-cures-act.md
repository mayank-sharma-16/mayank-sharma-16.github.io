# Overview
The 21st Century Cures Act is a bipartisan law passed by the United States government that allocated $6.3 billion towards medical products and research. This included:

- The Cancer Moonshot: $1.8 billion towards research into the prevention, detection, and treatment of cancer

- The BRAIN Initiative: $1.51 billion towards technologies that map the human brain and guide the treatment of neurological and mental disorders

- The All of Us Research Program: $1.45 billion towards health data collection from over a million volunteers to help personalize treatment protocols

- The Regenerative Medicine Innovation Project: $30 million towards adult stem cell research while accelerating approval pathways for Regenerative Medical Advanced Therapies (RMAT)

- Allowing the FDA to evaluate data outside of clinical trials, such as [[electronic-health-records|Electronic Health Records (EHRs)]] and patient registries
