# Overview
The North American Syringe Exchange Network (NASEN) is the first and largest distributor of low-cost [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] specialist and supporter of [[syringe-services-program|syringe services programs (SSPs)]] in the United States. It was founded by the [[dave-purchase-project|Dave Purchase Project]] in 1992, and it maintains a directory on its website that includes over 590 harm-reduction locations and SSPs. [^0]
