# Overview
The Recovery Leadership Summit is hosted annually by Faces & Voices of Recovery through the [[alliance-for-recovery-centered-organizations|Alliance for Recovery-Centered Organizations (ARCO)]] program. It allows advocates, practitioners, state and federal agencies, policymakers, researchers, and leaders of Recovery Community Organizations (RCOs) to meet and discuss the development of [[recovery|recovery]] infrastructure and programs.
