# Overview
Peer Galaxy is a platform that aggregates [[peer-recovery|peer-led recovery]] services and events across the world with a focus on Oregon. From their main page, they maintain peer support, recovery and wellness activities, webinars, consumer input, and special events. They also focus on addressing barriers to participation through [[accessibility|accessibility]] and translation options that are commonly a secondary consideration for both online and offline groups.
