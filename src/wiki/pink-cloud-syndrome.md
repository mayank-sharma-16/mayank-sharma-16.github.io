# Overview
Pink cloud syndrome is a term that was coined in Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) circles that describes the optimism and even euphoria that one can feel during the early stages of sobriety. It's often called a "honeymoon phase" in the context of a recovery timeline. It can be a trend that increases motivation and resilience, but it also risks overconfidence that leads one to drop their coping mechanism or support groups. Many who have a pink cloud phase end up relapsing because a sober life ends up being more mundane or disappointing than they initially felt.
