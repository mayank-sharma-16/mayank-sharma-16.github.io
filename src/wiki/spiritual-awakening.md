# Overview
The idea of a "spiritual awakening" has existed in some form in virtually every culture to describe a deep change in someone's perspectives that results from a new understanding of the world or the self.

In recovery circles, the term was popularized by 12-step groups like Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) to discuss the psychological and spiritual changes someone might experience as a result of doing the steps and losing their obsession with continuing past patterns of substance use. For the vast majority of those in 12-step programs, spiritual awakenings will be gradual changes over time rather than sudden transformations.
