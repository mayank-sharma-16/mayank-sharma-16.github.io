# Overview
The University of Southern California's Institute for Addiction Science (IAS) claims to be the first university-wide transdisciplinary institute for [[addiction|addiction]] research and education in the United States. They also run an annual [[early-career-addiction-science-showcase|Early Career Addiction Science Showcase (ECASS)]] for undergraduate students, graduate students, and junior faculty to present research before publication.
