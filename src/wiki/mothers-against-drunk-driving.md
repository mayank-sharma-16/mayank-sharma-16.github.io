# Overview
Mothers Against Drunk Driving (MADD) is America's largest grassroots non-profit dedicated to the issues of [[driving-under-influence|drunk driving]] and underage drinking. It has [[lobbying|lobbied]] for over 1000 laws across the United States at the local, state, and federal levels, notably setting the legal drinking age to 21 and the permitted [[blood-alcohol-concentration|Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC)]] limit for driving to 0.08%. Recently, MADD is lobbying for the passage of the HALT Act (Honoring Abbas Family Legacy to Terminate Drunk Driving) which would require [[impairment|impairment]] detection technology in every new passenger vehicle by 2030. Internal metrics show that MADD's efforts have cut drunk driving fatalities by up to 51%, which means 500,000 lives have been saved since 1980, and about 1 million victims of crashes with impaired drivers have received legal, financial, or emotional assistance.
