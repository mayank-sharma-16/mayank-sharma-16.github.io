# Overview
Rumination is the process of repeatedly replaying negative events or possibilities in ways that don't productively drive at a solution. It's a major symptom in depression, [[anxiety|anxiety]], Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD), and Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD). Developing the coping skills and structures to reduce isolation and prevent rumination is a common theme in recovery circles and literature. Rumination-Focused Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (RF-CBT) is dedicated [[cognitive-behavioral-therapy|CBT]] modality to address the process and function of rumination.
