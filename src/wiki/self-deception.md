# Overview
Self-deception is the act of denying reality consciously or unconsciously, usually as a defense mechanism for the ego and one's capacity to tolerate anxiety. Common mechanisms of self-deception include:

- Denial: refusal to acknowledge reality

- Repression: blocking out intolerable thoughts and feelings

- Rationalization: creating convenient or comfortable explanations or excuses

- Projection: attributing one's thoughts and feelings to others

- Emotional reasoning

- [[selective-attention|Selective attention]]

- Overgeneralization

- Self-Serving Bias: the tendency to attribute one's success to skill and choices while attributing failures to bad luck
