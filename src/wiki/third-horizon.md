# Overview
Third Horizon is a healthcare advisory firm that offers solutions in the spaces of behavioral health and [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]. Specifically, they address strategy, pricing, data analytics, and actuarial modeling for nonprofits, health systems, and government agencies. The President as of 2026 is [[greg-d-williams|Greg D. Williams]], a filmmaker and activist in recovery himself. 

Its projects include collaboration with the Mental Health Parity Index (MHPI), a tool to evaluate the gaps between mental healthcare and physical healthcare, and the Alliance for Addiction Payment Reform, which tries to replace traditional fragmented billing processes that are meant for acute crisis care rather than long-term recovery. They developed a value-based framework called The Addiction Recovery Medical Home - Alternative Payment Model (ARMH-APM). [^0]
