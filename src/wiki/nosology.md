# Overivew
Nosology is the field of medicine that tries to systemically classify diseases with operational definitions and characteristics. There are three general methodologies for classifcation:

- Categorical Models: diseases are distinct entities, and patients either meet the criteria for a disease or don't.

- Dimensional Models: health is a spectrum rather than a set of states where someone either has an illness or not. 

- Prototype Matching Models: diseases follow archetypes with some variation, and clinicians assess how close a patient's presentation mirrors them.

The most common examples of classification systems include the International Classification of Diseases (ICD), Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM), Medical Subject Headings (MeSH), and Research Domain Criteria (RDoC).
