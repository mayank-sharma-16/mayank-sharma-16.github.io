# Overview
The Florida Center for Prevention Research (FCPR) is an applied research center at Florida State University founded in 1998, initially to evaluate the Florida Tobacco Pilot Program. Since then, it has provided data analytics for the FLORENCE Project to reduce fatal [[overdose|overdoses]], measured the prevalence of [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]], coordinated local substance prevention programs like the Wakulla Drug-Free Communities Initiative, and hosted family and marital stabilization workshops.
