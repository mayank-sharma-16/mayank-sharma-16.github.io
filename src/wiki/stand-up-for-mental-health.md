# Overview
Stand Up for Mental Health (SMH) is a comedy therapy program that trains people with mental health diagnoses to practice stand-up comedy and build confidence in public, founded by David Granirer in 2004. Participants write stand-up routines about their personal experiences and perform shows for mental health organizations, military bases, colleges, universities, corporations, and general audiences.  One of the goals is to challenge stigma by presenting individuals with mental health conditions as funny and [[resilience|resilient]] rather than volatile or weird, as [[stereotype|stereotypes]] might show. They operate in over 50 cities across Canada, the United States, and Australia. The documentaries "Cracking Up" and "Crack Up" have focused on the organization's work to combat [[stigma|stigma]] in Canada and Australia, respectively. [^0]
