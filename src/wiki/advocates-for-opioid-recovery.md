# Overview
Advocates for Opioid Recovery (AOR) is a non-profit founded in 2016 as a bipartisan effort to advance evidence-based treatments for [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]], launched by Patrick J. Kennedy, Newt Gingrich, and Van Jones.
