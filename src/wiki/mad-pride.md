# Overview
Mad Pride is a movement to reclaim the term "mad" and "madness" as part of a challenge to institutional norms about mental health and celebration of neurological diversity. Generally, those in the Mad Pride movement believe that the [[lived-experience|lived experiences]] of those who have undergone psychiatric treatment and institutionalization should be prioritized in models of care. Forcing institutionalization and discriminating against people on the basis of mental differences are seen as human and civil rights issues. Although the centralized movement was mainly active from 1993 to 2012, plenty of groups and communities continue to identify with Mad Pride.
