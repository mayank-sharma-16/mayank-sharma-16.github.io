# Overview
Neglect has both medical and legal contexts that are related in that they both describe a caregiver abandoning an obligation to help someone dependent on them.

The DSM-5 has a section called "Other Conditions That May Be a Focus of Clinical Attention" with a subsection "Abuse and Neglect" that includes Child Neglect, Parent-Child Relational Problems, Child Affected by Parental Relationship Distress, and Child Psychological Abuse. These aren't considered mental disorders, but identifying them can guide treatment plans and add context for complications in how a disorder presents itself.
