# Overview
Telehealth refers to medical care delivered through technology that enables remote access. This includes telemedicine, remote patient monitoring (RPM), store-and-forward, and e-visits and messaging portals.

After the COVID pandemic, telehealth services saw a huge expansion in both the industry sector and public adoption. There are lots of potential benefits from telehealth for rural or remote populations that might lack access to nearby care, but those in rural and remote areas are also less likely to have the infrastructure to support reliable digital connections.
