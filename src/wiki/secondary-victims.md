# Overview
A secondary victim is someone who suffers harm as a result of witnessing or being otherwise influenced by a traumatic event that harmed a primary victim. These harms can include depression, [[anxiety|anxiety]], and Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD). In healthcare settings, this can extend to healthcare workers who suffer trauma or depression after harm befalls a patient. The term is distinct from secondary victimization, which refers to the additional harm caused to someone as a result of mistreatment from emergency, healthcare, or accountability systems.
