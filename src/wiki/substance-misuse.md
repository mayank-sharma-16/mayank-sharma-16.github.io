# Overview
"Substance misuse" refers to the use of medications or legal substances in ways that don't follow standard guidelines. It was proposed as an alternative to [[substance-abuse|"substance abuse,"]] whose negative connotations impacted the way medical professionals and the public perceived those who struggle with Substance Use Disorders (SUDs) or suffered health consequences from their substance use patterns. This includes cases like elderly patients accidentally taking multiple doses of their medications due to forgetfulness or use someone else's prescription in the case of an untreated injury.
