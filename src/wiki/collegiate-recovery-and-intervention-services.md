# Overview
The Collegiate Recovery and Intervention Services (CRIS) under the Collegiate Recovery Community (CRC) at the University of Alabama offers both clinical interventions and an [[abstinence|abstinence-based]] [[recovery|recovery]] community. Their services include:

- First Steps Program: early intervention for students experiencing low or moderate negative consequences from their substance use

- Insight Program: individualized clinical intervention and strategies for navigating campus life

- University Community Program (UCP): an intensive outpatient intervention designed for students who need structure and external accountability

- Community Campus Drug Court Program: an intensive [[drug-court|drug court]] [[diversion|diversion]] program for students facing offenses related to substance use

- Specialized Clinical Care: screenings, counseling, and other direct support for students struggling with SUDs, [[eating-disorder|eating disorders]], or problem gambling
