# Overview
The Twelve Steps are a guide to practicing recovery principles created by Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) and replicated by other anonymous mutual-aid societies built around the idea of peer fellowship, like Narcotics Anonymous (NA) and Gamblers Anonymous (GA). About half of all rehabilitation centers in the United States use 12-step programs in their baseline approach to treatment. Specialized therapeutic approaches have also been designed around 12-step fellowship, like [[twelve-step-facilitation|Twelve-Step Facilitation]].
