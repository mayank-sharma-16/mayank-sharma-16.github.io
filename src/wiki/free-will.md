# Overview
"Free will" refers to the philosophical concept that living things have the ability to make decisions based on independent agency. 

One of the most common [[neuroethics|neuroethical]] controversies surrounding [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] is over the extent to which an individual's agency is compromised both by the influence of substances in active addiction and intense cravings even when sober.
