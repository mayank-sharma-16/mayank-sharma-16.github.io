# Overview
Tobacco-containing tobacco product (TCTP) is a term that arose because of a quirk in the way that definitions are used in regulations in the United States. Under the Family Smoking Prevention and Tobacco Control Act, the Food and Drugs Administration defines a "tobacco product" as anything derived from tobacco, including synthetic nicotine. This means that e-cigarettes and tobacco-free nicotine pouches are still legally classified as "tobacco products." The separate term TCTP arose to differentiate products that contained nicotine from products that contained raw plant matter (cigarettes, cigars, snus, pipe tobacco, etc.)
