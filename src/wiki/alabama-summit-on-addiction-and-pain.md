# Overview
The Alabama Summit on Addiction and Pain (ASAP) began in 2024 while sponsored by the [[center-for-addiction-and-pain-prevention-and-intervention|Center for Addiction and Pain Prevention and Intervention (CAPPI)]] to address the intersection of chronic pain and [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]. This includes looking at the physiological mechanisms of pain signaling and experiencing, non-addictive treatments like non-[[hallucinogenic|hallucinogenic]] derivatives of ibogaine and [[psilocybin|psilocybin]], and pain care as a fundamental human right.
