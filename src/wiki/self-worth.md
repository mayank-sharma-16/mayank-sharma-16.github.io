# Overview
Self-worth is someone's sense of being good enough to be deserving of respect as a human being. It's distinct from [[self-esteem|self-esteem]] in that self-worth is about the belief in one's inherent value as a human being while self-esteem is about the feelings someone has towards themselves. For instance, someone's self-esteem about their skills might fluctuate based on their recent performance, but that may not impact their self-worth that says they still deserve to be treated like an equal human being.
