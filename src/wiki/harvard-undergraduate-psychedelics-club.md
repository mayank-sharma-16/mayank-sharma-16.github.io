# Overview
The Harvard Undergraduate Psychedelics Club (HUPC) is a student-run organization that explores and advocates for the usage of [[psychedelics|psychedelics]], including cultural and spiritual growth. Their initiatives include the "Psychedelic Week at Harvard" that presents scientific research and [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] protocols to the public.
