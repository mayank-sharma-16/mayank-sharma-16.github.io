# Overview
The National Black Harm Reduction Network (HBHRN) is the first Black-led [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] organization in the United States, which formalized a decades-old mutual aid and volunteer collective that conducted [[syringe-services-program|syringe exchanges]] in predominantly Black communities. Its work includes opioid settlement advocacy, "Reclaiming Leadership" to promote diversity in public health leadership, national campaigns like "Black Parents Against Overdose," [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] distribution campaigns like "You Have the Power to Save Lives," technical assistance (TA) to local organizations that provide harm-reduction services, and argue against systemic disparities in public health policy and drug policing.
