# Overview
The CCAR Recovery Coach Academy (RCA) is a 5-day training program approved by national boards like [[naadac-the-association-for-addiction-professionals|NAADAC]]. Although it's not a license or state certification program itself, its curriculum can satisfy the educational hour requirements of formal credentials for positions like a Certified Peer Recovery Specialist (CPRS) or a Certified Recovery Coach (CRC).
