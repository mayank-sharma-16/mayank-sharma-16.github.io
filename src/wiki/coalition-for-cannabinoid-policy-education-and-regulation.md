# Overview
The Coalition for Cannabinoid Policy, Education, and Regulation (CPEAR) is a non-profit dedicated to advocating for a science-driven regulatory framework for cannabis and [[cannabinoids|cannabinoids]]. It brings together industry representatives, academia, think tanks, public safety officials, medical professionals, financial services firms, and social justice organizations. [^0]
