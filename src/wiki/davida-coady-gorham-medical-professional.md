# Overview
The Davida Coady Gorham Medical Professional of the Year Award was renamed in 2019 from the [[NAADAC|naadac-the-association-for-addiction-profesisonals]] Medical Professional of the Year Award. 

Dr. Davida Coady Gorham practiced medicine internationally before settling down with a focus on [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] treatment. She was a first responder in humanitarian crises in Bangladesh, Cambodia, and India, where Mother Teresa invited her to be a medical director. Instead, she came back to America, specifically California, and founded Options Recovery Services as a [[diversion|diversion]] treatment program for the Berkeley Court in 1997, which expanded to provide evidence-based treatment and [[sober-living-environment|sober living opportunities]] to thousands of people. In 2006, Options Recovery Services expanded to work with the California prison system and trained hundreds of incarcerated individuals to become certified addiction treatment [[counseling|counselors]].

The award was named posthumously in her honor, and eligibility requires a minimum of three years of work in addiction treatment, a medical professional license, a focus on fostering long-term recovery with direct patient contact, and adherence to NAADAC ethical codes.
