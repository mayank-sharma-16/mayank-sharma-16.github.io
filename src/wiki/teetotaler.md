---

# Overview
A teetotaler is a person who refrains from drinking any type of alcohol. The word is often seen as old-fashioned compared to terms like "sober" or "non-drinker."

# Historical Context
In the 1800s, "tee-total" was regional English and Americna Slang used as an intensifier, in the same way as "entirely" or "completely." In 1833, Richard "Dicky" Turner, a reformer with a speech impediment, gave a speech against moderation that included the phrase "t-t-total abstinence." It was quickly adopted by the abstinence-only factions of the temperance movements, with some creating "T-Total" pledges to demonstrate commitment to total abstinence.
