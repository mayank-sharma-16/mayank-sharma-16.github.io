# Overview
The United States Conference of Mayors addresses [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] through a Substance Abuse, Prevention and Recovery Task Force. Findings from this task force includes:

- 97% of cities reported a signifiance increase in the demand for mental health services over a 2-year period 

- 85% of cities identified substance use as the main factor in the growing need for local services

- 88% of cities report an inadequate access to mental health and substance use recovery resources

- 56% of cities report severe shortages of qualified mental health workers and behavorial health facilities

- of the 20 cities experiencing the highest [[overdose|overdose]] rates, 45% of their mayors identify substance use as their most destructive health challenge
