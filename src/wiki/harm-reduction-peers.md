# Overview
The Harm Reduction Peers (HaRPs) are a cohort of [[peer-recovery|peer volunteers]] who perform outreach and education. That includes [[overdose|overdose]]-reversal workshops, [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] distribution, and pop-ups about myths surrounding substance use.
