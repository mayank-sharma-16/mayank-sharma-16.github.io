# Overview
Sterilization has two main definitions:

- the process of cleansing something of undesirable compounds or organisms

- the process of removing an organism's reproductive abilities

In the United States, eugenics laws of the 20th century led to forced sterilization of thousands of people with disabilities, including those who struggled with substance use disorder and were labeled [[habitual-drunkenness|"habitual drunkards,"]] [[inebriety|"inebriates,"]] and "mentally unfit."
