# Overview
Voices for Non-Opioid Choices is a bipartisan national organizations dedicated to preventing [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]] by advocating for alternative pain treatments to opioid medications or devices. The organization [[lobbying|lobbied]] Congress for the passage of the Non-Opioids Prevent Addiction in the Nation (NOPAIN) Act, which allowed Medicare reimbursement on non-opioid pain management options for outpatient surgery.
