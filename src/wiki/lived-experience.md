# Overview
"Lived experience" is a term that's been used a lot in online discourse in the debates about the value of evidence between statistics and personal anecdotes. Sometimes it's used to dismiss evidence provided by statistics when they don't match someone's expectations of reality, but it's also fairly used to describe the failure of statistics to offer a complete model of reality. Philosophers, particularly in phenomenology, approach lived experience with a critical examination over consciousness and interpretations of reality. For researchers in the social sciences, measuring lived experiences requires qualitative methods like constructing ethnographies.
