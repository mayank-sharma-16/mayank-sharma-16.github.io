# Overview
Sexually transmitted infections (STIs)  are infections that are commonly transmitted through sexual activity. The term is used interchangeably with sexually transmitted disease (STD) in everyday life, but technically they have different uses. STI is used when something has entered the body even if there are no symptoms, as is the case much of the time. STD is used when an infection has caused health issues or symptoms. 

Common STIs include:

- Chlamydia

- Gonorrhea

- Syphilis

- Trichomoniasis

- Human papillomavirus (HPV)

- Genital Herpes (HSV)

- Hepatitis B

- HIV
