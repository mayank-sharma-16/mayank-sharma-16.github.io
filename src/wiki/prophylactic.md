# Overview
A prophylactic is a medical treatment that prevents a condition from occurring. Examples include vaccines, pre-surgery antibiotics, [[post-exposure-prophylaxis|PEP]], [[pre-exposure-prophylaxis|PrEP]], and preventative mastectomies and hysterectomies.

Within treatment for substance use, prophylactics include:

- [[antagonist|Antagonists]]: medications like Naltrexone block opioid receptors and the euphoric feeling that accompanies the use of opioids or alcohol; Naloxone can reverse overdoses that cause fatal respiratory depression

- Anti-Craving Medication: medications like Acamprosate can help rebalance the disrupted glutamate and GABA systems caused by chronic alcohol use.

- Relapse Prevention: coping skills and self-maintenance principles, usually based on Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy
