# Overview
The Maryknoll Mission is a Catholic non-profit network founded in the United States in 1911. It consists of four independent but collaborative organization: Maryknoll Fathers & Brothers, Maryknoll Sisters, Maryknoll Lay Missioners, and Maryknoll Affiliates. Local missioners often manage support services for those with addictions, like Megan Hamilton who started the first structured Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) meetings in regional clinics in Mombasa, Kenya. [^0] They're an example of [[wraparound-services|wraparound services]] whose work supports the treatment provided by medical institutions.
