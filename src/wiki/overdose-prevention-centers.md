# Overview
Overdose Prevention Centers (OPCs), also called Overdose Prevention Sites (OPS) and Safer Drug Consumption Services (SCS), are places where someone can use substances they've acquired with professionals on-site to act in case of an overdose. They're part of evidence-based [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]] strategies that try to reduce the strain on emergency services by keeping populations who might need it nearby.
