# Overview
Faces & Voices of Recovery is a national non-profit that mobilizes a network of Recovery Community Organizations (RCOs). Their projects include advocacy with lawmakers and public institutions, certification through the Council on Accreditation of Peer Recovery Support Services (CAPRSS), [[peer-recovery|peer-run]] education, training and technical assistance (TTA), and public messaging campaigns like "The Anonymous People."
