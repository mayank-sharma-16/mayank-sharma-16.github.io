# Overview
The Coalition for Whole Health is a national coalition of over 150 organizations with the goal of ensuring federal health care laws fully fund and include mental health disorders, including substance use prevention, treatment, and [[recovery|recovery]]. It's managed under the [[legal-action-center|Legal Action Center (LAC)]], a non-profit for law and policies concerning mental health.
