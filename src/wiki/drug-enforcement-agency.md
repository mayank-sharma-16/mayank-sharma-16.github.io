# Overview
The Drug Enforcement Agency (DEA) is a law enforcement agency of the United States Department of Justice tasked with enforcing the Controlled Substances Act and similar policies regulating substances. 

It operates 241 domestic offices and 93 foreign offices across 110 countries to target drug trafficking organizations with raids against money launderers, organized criminal gangs, and [[narcoterrorism|narco-terrorists]] with a Special Operations Division (SOD) that works alongside global intelligence agencies. It also establishes strict quotes for how much of a controlled substance can be legally manufactured in the United States to reduce the potential supply for recreational use and make the existing supply easier to monitor. The agency's forensic laboratories test seized materials to analyze their chemical structures, including [[designer-drugs|emerging synthetic drugs]] like [[nitazenes|nitazenes]] and trace their origins through [[black-market|black markets]] geographically.
