# Overview
Recovery-oriented care (ROC) is a framework for mental health and substance use treatment that focuses on an individual's overall quality of life and integration into a community rather than just symptom reduction. 

The Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) proposes four dimensions to recovery under the Recovery Support Strategic Initiative: health, home, purpose, and community. It also identifies ten guiding principles of recovery: hope, person-driven, many pathways, holistic, peer support, relational, culture, addresses trauma, strengths/responsibility, and respect. SAMHSA serves as one of the central institutions in the United States that promotes ROCs by incentivizing the creation of [[recovery-oriented-systems-of-care|Recovery-Oriented Systems of Care (ROSC)]].
