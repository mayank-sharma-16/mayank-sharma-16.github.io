# Overview

The APA Dictionary of Psychology defines "learning" as

*"the acquisition of new information, behaviors, or abilities after practice, observation, or other experiences, as evidenced by change in behavior, knowledge, or brain function. Learning involves attending to relevant aspects of incoming information, organizing the information into a coherent cognitive representation, and integrating it with relevant existing knowledge from long-term memory."* [^0]
