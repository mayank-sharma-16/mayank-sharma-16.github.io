# Overview
A slip, also called a lapse, refers to a minor break in sobriety or temporary return to the behavior of active addiction. Although it's a matter of some subjectivity, a slip implies a single moment or day that's followed by an immediate return to routine. Maybe someone accidentally has an alcoholic drink at a party without realizing it, or maybe someone used a friend's joint one evening at a party and has continued to be sober for weeks afterward. These slips or lapses are not considered a huge deal as long as they're quickly corrected and normal routines are sustained, though it does add pressure for those who count the number of consecutive days they've been sober.
