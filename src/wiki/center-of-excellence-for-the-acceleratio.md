# Overview
The Center of Excellence for the Acceleration of Harm Reduction was established at the University of Catania in 2018 to evaluate the strategies that reduce the health risks associated with smoking. That includes studying [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] alternatives like [[e-cigarettes|e-cigarettes]] and [[comorbidities|comorbidities]] with conditions like [[diabetes|diabetes]].
