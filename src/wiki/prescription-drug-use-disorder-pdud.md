# Overview
Prescription drug use disorder (PDUD) isn't an official diagnosis itself, but the term can be found in research literature and surveys when estimating how many people struggle with substance use disorders that specifically stem from prescription drugs. The exact chemical nature and bodily effects of prescription drugs vary a lot from one to another, so it's not very useful from a medical perspective to have a subcategory of SUD based solely on the method that the substances were obtained through. However, from policymaking and public health perspectives, that distinction can be useful in understanding the process through which pharmaceutials approved by medical professionals end up misused.
