# Overview
The American Association for the Treatment of Opioid Dependence (AATOD) is a non-profit dedicated to expanding access to medication-assisted treatment for [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]] and representing over 1400 [[opioid-treatment-program|Opioid Treatment Programs (OTPs)]]. They also host national conferences every 18 months to discuss evidence-based clinical practices and breakthroughs in addiction medicine. [^0]
