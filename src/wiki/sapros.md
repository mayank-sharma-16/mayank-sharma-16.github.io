# Overview
Social Aspects Public Relations Organizations (SAPRO) are interest groups funded by industries to facilitate [[corporate-social-responsibility|corporate social responsibility (CSR)]] obligations and favorable public relations. They tend to argue for personal responsibility over industry accountability, lobby on public health measures in ways that favor industry self-regulation, and promote narratives that often promote industry-friendly media campaigns and criticize policies that threaten their profits. While traditional trade associations engage heavily with commercial lobbying, SAPROs refer specifically to organizations that target medical discourse and public health circles.
