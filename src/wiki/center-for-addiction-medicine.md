# Overview
The Center for Addiction Medicine (CAM) within Massachusetts General Hospital is affiliated with Harvard Medical School. They investigate substance use through:

- Addiction Recovery Management Service (ARMS): supports teens, young adults, and families in navigating [[recovery|recovery]] from [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]

- West End Clinic: outpatient clinic for adults dealing with SUDs and co-occurring conditions like [[depression|depression]] and [[anxiety|anxiety]]

- iDECIDE Project: educational alternative to suspension for middle and higher schoolers using substances on school campuses

- Recovery Research Institute (RRI): manages platforms to share addiction science and evaluate recovery programs at schools and colleges

- trials into [[peer-recovery|peer recovery coaching]], vaping cessation, intersections between serious mental illness (SMI) and [[tobacco|tobacco]] usage, and [[nicotine|nicotine]] reduction through Accelerated Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation
