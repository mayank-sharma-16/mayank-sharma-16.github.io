# Overview
Opioids are a class of drugs that bind to opioid receptors in the brain and body, which can block pain signals and cause feelings of [[euphoria|euphoria]]. Common examples include [[codeine|codeine]], [[fentanyl|fentanyl]], and [[heroin|heroin]]. Chronic use increases the risk of higher [[tolerance|tolerance]], [[dependence|dependence]], and [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]]. [[harm-reduction|Harm-reduction]] frameworks frequently include [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] distribution to reverse opioid [[overdose|overdoses]].
