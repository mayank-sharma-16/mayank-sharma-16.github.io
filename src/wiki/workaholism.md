# Overview
Workaholism is a compulsive need to work or inability to disengage from work for reasons other than financial or job requirements. It's not classified as an official disease or disorder in the DSM-5-TR or the ICD-11, but it still describes a pattern of behavior that clinicians and treatment providers take seriously. It can be a marker of Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder (OCPD), [[depression|depression]], [[anxiety|anxiety]], and [[attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder|ADHD]].
