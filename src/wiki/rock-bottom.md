# Overview
The idiom "rock bottom" refers to the lowest possible condition something or someone can be in. It comes from 19th-century mining industries as a phrase that referred to the hardest layers they could hit when digging down, and it was often adapted for slogans like "rock-bottom prices." It was popularized by Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) in reference to a low point someone encounters in active addiction from which they might derive a [[gift-of-desperation|gift of desperation]] that motivates drastic and lasting change.
