# Overview
Greg D. Williams is a documentary filmmaker who covers [[addiction|addiction]] and its [[stigma|stigma]] while supporting national advocacy initiatives in the United States. His personal struggles with substance use began with [[alcohol|alcohol]] and [[cannabis|cannabis]] around the age of 13, followed by [[opioid|opioids]] and [[benzodiazepines|benzodiazepines]] by 16, but he committed to sobriety at 17 after treatment. 

In 2015, Williams served as the campaign director and executive producer for the UNITE to Face Addiction Rally at the National Mall in Washington, D.C. He also co-founded the non-profit Facing Addiction before it merged with [[national-council-on-alcoholism-and-drug-dependence|National Council on Alcoholism and Drug Dependence (NCADD)]].
