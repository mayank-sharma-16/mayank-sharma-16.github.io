# Overview
The Michigan State University Collegiate Recovery community (CRC) offers a campus environment for students seeking recovery from [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]]. Their services include recovery-focused housing, [[peer-recovery|peer support]] meetings, 24/7 student lounge, administering the Jamie Daniels Memorial Scholarship, Recovery Ally Training, sober recreational activities, and [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]] through [[overdose|overdose]] prevention training and [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] distribution. Data from the MSU National College Health Assessment Survey shows that 3.2% (1600) of campus members identify as being in recovery.
