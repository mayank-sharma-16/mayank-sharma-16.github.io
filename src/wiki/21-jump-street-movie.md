# Overview
21 Jump Street (2012) is a comedy based on the [[21-jump-street-show|show]] featuring undercover cops using their youthful appearance to infiltrate drug dealers and find the suppliers at a local high school. The movie uses a fictional [[designer-drug|synthetic drug]] called HFS, short for "Holy Fucking Shit", though it functions mostly as a plot device to bring the protagonists back to the [[liminal|liminal]] space of high school. Schmidt and Jenko revisit the [[schema|schemas]] and sense of self-worth they developed in high school while they were teenagers on the path to adulthood, confronting the fickle nature of social hierarchies as a more progressive generation frowns upon Jenko's stereotypical jock and embraces Schmidt's offbeat yet earnest individualism.

The symptoms of using HFS are kind of a composite of the effects caused by real substances: 

1) The Giggs: [[euphoria|euphoria]], relaxation, distorted senses; mimicking the highs from [[cannabis|cannabis]]

2) Tripping Major Ballsack: [[psychoactive|psychoactive]] hallucinations or altered states of consciousness; similar to the effects of [[lsd|lsd]] or certain [[magic-mushrooms|mushrooms]]. 

3) Over-Falsity of Confidence: an episode that resembles the [[liquid-courage|liquid courage]] aspect of [[alcohol|alcohol]]

4) Fuck Yeah Motherfucker: angry, erratic, irrational, and violent tendencies; this represents some of the conventional ideas about the destructive episodes caused by [[hard-drugs|hard drugs]] or advanced stages of [[active-addiction|active addiction]]

5) Asleepyness: [[lethargy|lethargy]], [[fatigue|fatigue]], and [[catatonia|catatonia]]; this represents the [[comedown|comedown]] effects that accompanies a lot of substances, where negative effects like low moods and exhaustion set in.
