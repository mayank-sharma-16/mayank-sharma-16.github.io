# Overview
The Office for Victims of Crime (OVC) is an agency under the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) that funds crime victime services across the country. This includes a $3.9 billion crime victims fund supported by criminal fines and forfeited bail bonds, [[training-and-technical-assistance|training and technical assistance (TTA)]] for state-level programs, and support for culturally-specific initiatives for indigenous Native American organizations. Its programs include compensation for [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] treatment, with the recognition that substance use is commonly an attempt to self-medicate following the impacts of [[trauma|trauma]].
