# Overview
The Office of Substance Use Programs Education & Resources (SUPER) at Stanford University's Vaden Health Services, focused on education, prevention, and [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]]. That includes providing counseling sessions, [[fentanyl-test-strips|fentanyl test strips]], [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]], "SUPER cups" that prevent drinks from being spiked, [[peer-recovery|peer-led]] workshops and trainings, and peer support networks for those with [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]].
