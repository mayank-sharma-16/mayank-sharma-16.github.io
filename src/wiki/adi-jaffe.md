# Overview
Adi Jaffe is a psychologist in recovery who founded IGNTD, an online recovery platform. His own history involves struggling with [[substance-use-disorders|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]] with [[methamphetamine|methamphetamine]] and [[alcohol|alcohol]] before earning degrees in psychology from the University of California, Los Angeles while researching [[addiction|addiction]]. Jaffe argues against the prevalence of [[shame|shame]] and [[abstinence|abstinence]]-only models in treatment, as addiction is often a [[maladaptive|maladaptive]] coping mechanism or attempt at [[self-medication|self-medicating]] another problem.
