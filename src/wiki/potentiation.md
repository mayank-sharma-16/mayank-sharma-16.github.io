# Overview
Potentiation refers to a process of making something more effective through synergy. 

When using substances, the brain experiences Long-Term Potentiation (LTP), which means synapses are strengthened as they experience activity. The cues surrounding substances become associated with the intense reward of the substance, and the brain's expectations lead to strong cravings. LTP describes the physiological mechanisms through which [[positive-reinforcement|positive reinforcement]] is encoded in the brain. Recovery from substance use disorders involves sustained sobriety in order to weaken the potentiated pathways associated with using substances and strengthening those associated with new coping skills or experiences. [[contingency-management|Contingency Management]] offers immediate positive reinforcement to incentivize the potentiation of drug-free habits while the brain is still healing from a "hijacked" reward system.
