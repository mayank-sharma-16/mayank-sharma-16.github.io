# Overview
Terpin, also called Elixir of Terpin Hydrate (ETH), is a solution with high alcohol concentrations and opioid antitussives. It was sold [[over-the-counter|over-the-counter (OTC)]] as a cough medication until the 1990s in the United States, when the federal Food and Drugs Administration removed its status as "Generally Recognized as Safe and Effective" (GRASE) while citing a lack of modern evidence supporting its effectiveness as an expectorant. It has largely been substituted with guaifenesin, though it is still available in certain compounding pharmacies.

# Historical Context
Around WWII, it was referrede to as "G.I. Gin."
