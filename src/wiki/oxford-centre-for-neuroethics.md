# Overview
The Oxford Centre for Neuroethics was established in 2009 at the University of Oxford to explore the ethical, legal, and social impacts of breakthroughs in neuroscience. Their focus areas include the ethics of cognitive enhancement, [[consciousness|consciousness]], [[free-will|free will]], and morality in decision-making.
