# Overview
A Philosophical and Statistical History of the Inventions and Customs of Ancient and Modern Nations in the Manufacture and Use of Inebriating Liquors (1838) is the second edition of an 1824 essay by Samuel Morewood that studies [[alcohol|alcohol]] production and consumption across ancient and modern societies. In addition to beer, wine, and [[hard-liquor|hard liquor]], it also touches on [[opium|opium]] and [[stimulants|stimulants]] in cultures that substitute them for alcoholic beverages.
