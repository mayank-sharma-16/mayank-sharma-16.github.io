# Overview
American Made (2017) is a film adaptation of the life of [[barry-seal|Barry Seal]], an airline pilot who smuggled drugs for the Medellin Cartel before becoming an informant for the United States Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA). His life and operations were used as inspiration in many action and thriller stories that center conspiracies involving US intelligence agencies, cartel trafficking, and Cold War politics. Though the movie is one of these and uses Seal's name, the director Doug Liman has described it as "a fun lie based on a true story" since it mixes up timelines and creates a sympathetic protagonist out of Seal, who was a seasoned criminal rather than a bored and simple family man.
