# Overview
The Center for Prevention Research and Development (CPRD) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) approaches public policy through capacity building, practice improvement, and data-driven decision-making. For instance, it administers the Illinois Youth Survey every two years to track the [[prevalence|prevalence]] of substance use among teenagers, packages data for local anti-drug coalitions, and partners with Prevention First to track the outcomes of prevention programs that take public funds.
