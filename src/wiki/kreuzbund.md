# Overview
Kreuzbund e.V. is a German self-help and mutual aid organization for those struggling with [[addiction|addiction]] and their families. They provide about 1200 to 1600 free, confidential groups across Germany for [[peer-recovery|peer discussion]] and [[mutual-aid|mutual aid]]. [^0]
