# Overview
Syphilis is a bacterial [[sexually-transmitted-infection|sexually transmitted infection (STI)]].

Untreated, the disease goes through four stages. In the primary stage, 10 to 90 days, it causes a single sore at the point that bacteria entered. In the secondary stage, a few weeks to months after the sore heals, it causes a rash and flu-like symptoms, like fever, sore throat, muscle aches, and fatigue. Then comes the latent stage where all signs and symptoms of the disease disappear for years. Finally, in the tertiary stage 10 to 30 years after the initial infection, it can cause severe damage to organs in the body that result in death.
