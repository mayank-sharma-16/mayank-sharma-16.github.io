# Overview
Alan Marlatt was a clinical psychologist and professor of psychology with the University of Washington who developed the cognitive-behavioral model of [[relapse-prevention|Relapse Prevention (RP)]] and introduced the [[abstinence-violation-effect|Abstinence Violation Effect (AVE)]] with [[judith-r-gordon|Judith R. Gordon]]
