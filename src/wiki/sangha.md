# Overview
Sangha is a Sanskrit word that means "company" or "community." In Buddhist contexts, including recovery networks like Refuge Recovery (RR) and Recovery Dharma (RD), the Sangha refers to a monastic community or fellowship. In modern practice, it generally means a fourfold community of monks, nuns, laymen, and laywomen. It's one of the Three Jewels alongside the Buddha and the Dharma.

For RD/RR, the Sangha functions analogously to the concept of meetings in Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) in terms of fellowship.
