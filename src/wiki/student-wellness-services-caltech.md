# Overview
The California Institute of Technology's Student Wellness Services (SWS) was established in 2016 after the university merged its standalone health and counseling clinics. Their services include primary medical care, preventative medicine, [[sexually-transmitted-infections|STI]] testing, laboratory services, psychotherapy, crisis intervention, group sessions, occupational therapy, suicide prevention training, and substance use education. They apply a framework based on [[brief-alcohol-screening-and-intervention-for-college-students|BASICS]] that considers [[harm-reduction]] rather than [[abstinence-only]] and consider common co-occurring disorders as part of their care, like [[anxiety|anxiety]] and [[depression|depression]]. If students have severe symptoms of [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]], the clinicians at SWS can make external referrals confidentially.
