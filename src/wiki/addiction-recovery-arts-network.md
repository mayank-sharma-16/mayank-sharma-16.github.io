# Overview
The Addiction Recovery Arts (ARA) Network is an organization that supports the field of [[recovery|recovery]] arts by building collaborative connections in the United Kingdom. It maintains the magazine Performing Recovery, which covers artwork that touches on the themes of [[addiction|addiction]], [[recovery|recovery]], [[trauma|trauma]], and [[neurodivergence|neurodivergence]].
