# Overview
Nitazenes are a class of synthetic opioids that were developed as experimental pain relievers that never made it to market due to the severe risk of overdose. However, since 2019, they've been found more often as contaminants in other street drugs as a means of extending the psychoactive effects cheaply. Their molecular structure is significantly different than fentanyl's, which means they can often get around regulations around manufacturing and testing.
