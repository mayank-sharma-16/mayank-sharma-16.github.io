# Overview
The Legal Action Center (LAC) is a non-profit that fights discrimination against those who involved with the criminal justice system, [[substance-use-disorder|substance use disorders]], and HIV/AIDS. They also manage the [[coalition-for-whole-health|Coalition for Whole Health.]] Their services include direct free legal assistance, impact litigation challenging systemic discrimination, and advocacy across drug policy, patient privacy, fair hiring practices, and sealing records.
