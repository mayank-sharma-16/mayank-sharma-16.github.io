# Overview
People Engaged in Recovery (PEIR) is a [[peer-recovery|peer-led]] nonprofit in Alabama for individuals and families impacted by substance use disorders. They offer services like peer counseling, [[relapse-prevention|relapse prevention]] planning, support groups, and wellness activities. They also hosted a 100-mile "Enduring Their Legacies" walk that ended at the Alabama State Capitol in honor of those who [[overdose|overdosed]] and the families impacted by them.
