# Overview
Buprenorphine is a partial [[opioid|opioid]] [[agonist|agonist]] that relieves cravings and [[withdrawal|withdrawal]] symptoms from [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]] without causing [[euphoria|euphoria]]. It can be prescribed for [[medication-for-opioid-use-disorder|Medication for Opioid Use Disorder (MOUD)]] in the United States by any clinician licensed by the [[drug-enforcement-agency|Drug Enforcement Agency (DEA)]]. Brand names include Suboxone, Sublocade, and Brixadi.
