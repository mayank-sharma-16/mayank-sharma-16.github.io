# Overview
The Drug Policy Alliance (DPA) is a national non-profit heardquartered in New York City, New York with the goal of ending the war on drugs and advocating for evidence-based policies instead, like [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]]. Their advocacy has included:

- unifying a network of [[syringe-services-program|syringe service programs (SSPs)]]

- leading a campaign for marijuana decriminalization, especially calling attention to racial disparities in arrests

- supporting the expansion of [[overdose-prevention-centers|Overdose Prevention Centers (OPCs)]]

- lobbying against the Rockefeller Drug Laws that implemented strict [[mandatory-minimums|mandatory minimum]] sentencing guidelines in 1973
