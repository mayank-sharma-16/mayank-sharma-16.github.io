# Overview
According to current medical consensus, Viagra is not physically addictive, but it is possible to develop a psychological [[dependence|dependence]] on it such that lacking it can contribute to anxiety and performance issues. Stopping the medication does not cause physical sickness, and sildenafil, the active ingredient, doesn't activate the same pathways in the brain's reward center as substances associated with addiction normally do.

Viagra is commonly used for case studies in public health and health economic content for a few reasons. First, sex sells. Second, sex medication sells; because sex has a high and constant demand from people, viagra is a medication that is in constant production and distribution.
