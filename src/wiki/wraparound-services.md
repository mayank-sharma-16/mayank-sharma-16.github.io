# Overview
Wraparound services are the non-clinical community resources that complement clinical approaches to treating those with behavioral and mental health issues. These include housing services, transportation coordination, vocational training, job placement, educational advocacy, court liaison services, benefits navigation, Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) care coordination, nutritional counseling, childcare services, and [[peer-recovery|peer recovery]] support specialists.
