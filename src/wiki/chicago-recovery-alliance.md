# Overview
Chicago Recovery Alliance (CRA) is a non-profit that advocates for [[harm-reduction|harm reduction]] policies under the motto "any positive change." They pioneered Narcan ([[naloxone|Naloxone]]) distribution and operate mobile outreach programs across neighborhoods in Chicago. [^0]
