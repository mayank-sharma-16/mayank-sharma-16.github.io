# Overview
Aaron Strand is a filmmaker who uses his personal experiences with [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]] to drive his works. He graduated from New York University and began performing as an actor in New York City, during which his substance use escalated and led to homelessness in 2012. At the end of 2013, he returned to Athens, Georgia, his hometown, and entered recovery. While writing about different internal narratives he experiences in his [[active-addiction|active addiction]], he was encouraged by a college playwright friend to develop it into a monologue, which sparked his interest in filmmaking. He operates under a "NonDe" (non-dependent) filmmaking studio model where he toured the country to distribute his films to independent theaters while hosting live Q&A events.

# Works
In 2025, after a decade of sobriety, Strand made Withdrawal, a romantic semi-autobiographical drama about an artist couple detoxing from [[opioid|opioids]] to save their relationship. It was filmed in Athens, Georgia and sponsored by Nuci's Space, a local nonprofit resource center dedicated to addressing suicide and the stigma around mental health issues, primarily for musicians.
