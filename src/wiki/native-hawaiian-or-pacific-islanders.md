# Overview
In 1997, the U.S. Office of Management and Budget (OMB) issued a directive that separated the racial group "Asian" from "Native Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander" (NHPI or NHOPI), which refers to those whose origins are traced to Hawaii, Guam, American Samoa, Polynesia, Micronesia, and Melanesia. The category spans as many as 50 distinct ethnic groups.
