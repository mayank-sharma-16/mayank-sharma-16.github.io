# Overview
Students Against Destructive Decisions, which used to be known as Students Against Driving Drunk, is a national youth health and safety organization that addresses violence, mental health, and substance use. They have thousands of chapters in middle schools up through colleges in the United States, delivering their messages through a pledge called the "Contract for Life," peer-led events like National Youth Traffic Safety Month, corporate partnerships like State Farm for [[driving-under-influence|drunk driving]] simulators, and supporting youth leadership through a National Student Board of Directors.
