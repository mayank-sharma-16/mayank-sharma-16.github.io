# Overview
Tolerance is the pattern of a body adapting to chronic substance use such that larger doses are required over time to achieve the same effect. With repeated consumption of a substance, the liver and other organs become more efficient at clearing it from the bloodstream. Neurons adjust their sensitivity to chemical spikes by increasing or decreasing the number of receptors influenced by substance use. If someone stops using a substance for a period of time, their tolerance will likely drop rapidly. Unfortunately, in a relapse, many people will attempt to use the amount they last used despite the low tolerance, which means they might trigger an [[overdose|overdose]].
