# Overview
Volatile organic compounds (VOCs) are carbon-based chemicals with a high vapor pressure that easily evaporate into the air.

Many VOCs are part of inhalants that are targeted for recreational substance use. These include:

- Toluene: spray paint, model glue, rubber cement, and industrial solvents

- Nitrous oxide ("whippets"): whipped cream, chargers, medical anesthetics, automotive booster gas

- Aklyl nitries ([[poppers|"poppers"]]): leather cleaners, industrial solvents, video head cleaners

- Butane and Propane: lighter fluid, camping stove fuel, aerosol propellants

- Acetone: nail polish remover, industrial adhesives, paint strippers

- Tricholoroethylene: dry cleaning gluids, industrial degreasers, historical correction fluids
