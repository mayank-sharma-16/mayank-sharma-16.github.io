# Overview
The Opioid Settlement Fund Advisory Board was created in 2021 and operates under the New York State Office of Addiction Services and Supports (OASAS). Its goal is to manage settlement money obtained from legal cases against the supply chain for opioid manufacturing and distribution, and they usually recommend allocating funds towards substance use prevention, [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]], treatment, and [[recovery|recovery]] programs.[^0]
