# Overview
"Scrip doctors" is a term for doctors who illegally or irresponsibly write large numbers of prescriptions, which often end up being resold for recreational use. They might work out of a facility or center called a "pill mill," and the most common substances acquired through them include opioids like hydrocodone, muscle relaxants like Soma, and [[benzodiazepines|benzodiazepines]] like Xanax.
