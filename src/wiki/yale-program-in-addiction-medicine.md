# Overview
The Yale Program in Addiction Medicine (YPAM) operates as an interdisciplinary research initiative across the Yale School of Medicine. It manages an Addiction Recovery Clinic (ARC), consult services, specialized care like [[medication-for-opioid-use-disorder|M|Medication for Opioid Use Disorder (MOUD)]] through the New London Buprenorphine Clinic and the Opioid Reassessment Clinic. It also leads the National Methadone Access and Quality Commission (NMAQC), funded by the Foundation for Opioid Response Efforts (FORE), which advocates for policy reform that standardizes patient experiences with and access to [[methadone|methadone]].
