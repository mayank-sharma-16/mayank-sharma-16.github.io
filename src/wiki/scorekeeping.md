# Overview
Scorekeeping is a term that refers to tracking contributions, sacrifices, and offenses made in a relationship, usually to either gain the upper hand in an argument or track reciprocation. 

In [[recovery|recovery]] circles, scorekeeping is a habit associated with building [[resentment|resentment]] over time, and one of the alternative habits people in recovery often cultivate is giving without expectation of return. Many people who have the habit of scorekeeping grew up in critical environments where love seemed to be conditional and earned, and they may carry that forward into new relationships by viewing them as fundamentally transactional. This type of insecurity in attachments can interfere with conflict resolution since scorekeeping requires tracking who is right or wrong rather than just solving the conflict.
