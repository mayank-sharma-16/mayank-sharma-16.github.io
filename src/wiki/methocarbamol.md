# Overview
Methocarbamol is used as a muscle relaxant and pain reliever for health conditions that produce painful muscle spasms or cramps. As a central nervous system (CNS) depressant, it slows down nerve activity to provide relief. 

Side effects include:

- Dizziness and lightheadedness

- Drowsiness or fatigue

- Headache

- Blurred vision

- Nausea or upset stomach

Combining it with other substances like alcohol, opioids, or anxiety medications can compound the [[depressant|depressant]] effect.
