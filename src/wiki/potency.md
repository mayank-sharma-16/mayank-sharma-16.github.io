# Overview
Potency generally refers to something's capacity to act or strength of its effects. In pharmacology, potency can be measured in a few ways:

- Half-maximal Effective Concentration: the concentration of a substance that creates a response that's halfway between a baseline response and the maximum possible response

- Half-maximal Effective Dose: the dose of a substance that produces a particular intensity of effect in 50% of a test population.

- Half-maximal Inhibitory Concentration: the concentration of a substance that inhibits a biological process by 50%
