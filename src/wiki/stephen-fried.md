# Overview
Stephen Fried is a journalist and lecturer at the University of Pennsylvania and Columbia University.

# Works

- Bitter Pills: Inside the Hazardous World of Legal Drugs (1998)

- A Common Struggle: A Personal Journey Through the Past and Future of Mental Illness and Addiction (2015)

- Rush: Revolution, Madness, and Benjamin Rush, the Visionary Doctor Who Became a Founding Father (2018)

- Profiles in Mental Health Courage (2024)
