# Overview
Mobilize Recovery is a national non-profit that drives grassroots solutions to addressing mental health and [[substance-use-disorder|substance use disorders (SUDs)]]. It focuses on community organizing, political advocacy, and [harm-reduction|harm reduction]. Its programs have included Operation Understanding to honor historical recovery movements against systemic stigma, the Overdose Response Initiative to distribute [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]], Code Recover to bridge gaps between clinical care and local recovery pathways, Day of Service as an annual campaign to complete volunteer projects, and organizing national summits.
