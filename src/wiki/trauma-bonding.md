# Overview
In clinical psychology, "trauma bonds" refer to attachments that form between an abuser and their victim. In an abusive relationship, an abuser alternates between intense kindness and love bombing before demonstrating cruelty and manipulation. The cycle can be unpredictable and release neurotransmitters, dopamine and oxytocin, in a way that can feel addictive. Breaking the cycle comes with the anxiety of the unknown and, for many, complications to their financial and housing security.

In everyday conversation, people might use "trauma bonding" to mean bonding with someone over shared hardship, but the definitions in the field of psychology concern abusive relationships.
