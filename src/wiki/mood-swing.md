# Overview
"Mood swings" are a casual term for rapid shifts in emotional states rather than a specific clinical term. 

In clinical and research literature, there isn't much of a consensus for the definition of related terms like affective instability, which looks at someone's reactivity in relation to their environment; mood instability, which looks at the tendency to quickly experience distinct moods; and affective lability, which looks at the tendency to express intense shifts in emotional states. They're often used interchangeably, though the differences help delineate symptoms for distinct diagnoses. For instance, "affective instability" is better at describing the patterns of [[borderline-personality-disorder|Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD)]] while "mood instability" is better at describing the patterns of [[attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder|Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD).]]
