# Overview
The Guttempler, known before as the Good Templars or the Independent Order of Good Templars (IOGT), are the German branch of a global organization to address [[alcoholism|alcoholism]]. The original organization was founded in 1850 by Daniel Cady in Utica, New York and called the Knights of Jericho. It was renamed and reshaped, and it grew to arrive in Germany by 1889. With around 500 local groups, they form the largest religiously independent [[addiction|addiction]] self-help association in all of Germany. [^0]
