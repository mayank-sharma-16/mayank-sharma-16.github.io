# Overview
Gia Marie Carangi was an American supermodel who died at the age of 26 from [[aids|AIDS-related]] complications acquired after developing a [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] around [[heroin|heroin]].

As both one of the first supermodels and women to die from AIDS, her tragically early end has drawn attention as a notable casualty of the virus. Seven years after her death, [[stephen-fried|Stephen Fried]] wrote a biography about her life. Later, a dramatized biographical film of her life was made in 1998 called [[gia|Gia]].
