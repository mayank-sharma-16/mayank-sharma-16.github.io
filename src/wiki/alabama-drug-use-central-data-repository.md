# Overview
The Alabama Drug Use Central Data Repository (DrugUse-CDR) tracks substance use in Alabama with a particular focus on the [[opioid-crisis|opioid crisis]]. Some of their focus areas include deploying first responders to [[overdose|overdose]] out breaks, measuring the effectiveness of community initiatives, and providing the public with interactive dashboards. It was established in 2018 through the University of Alabama's Institute of Data and Analytics.
