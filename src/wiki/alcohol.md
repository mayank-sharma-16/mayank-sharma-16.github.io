# Overview
Alcohol in general conversation refers to [[ethanol|ethanol]], which is added to food or beverages as one of the world's most common recreational substances. Excessive drinking habits have gained a myriad of terms across the world across thousands of years of traditional rituals, recreational activities, and trauma followed by [[maladaptive|maladaptive]] coping mechanisms. These include [[alcoholism|alcoholism]], [[habitual-drunkenness|habitual drunkenness]], [[inebriety|inebriety]], and [[alcohol-use-disorder|Alcohol Use Disorder]].

In the context of chemistry, alcohol actually refers to a class of chemicals that includes ethanol and [[methanol|methanol]].
