# Overview
A syndrome is a collection of symptoms that occur together. It's distinct from a [[disease|disease]], which refers to a condition with a physiological cause, and a [[disorder|disorder]], which is a disruption in healthy functioning that may not have a known cause. 

Examples of syndromes related to substance use include:

- Alcohol Withdrawal Syndrome

- Delirium Tremens (DTs)

- Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome (NAS) and Neonatal Opioid Withdrawal Syndrome (NAWS)

- Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome

- Serotonin Syndrome

- Fetal Alcohol Syndrome
