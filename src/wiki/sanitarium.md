# Overview
A sanitarium (or sanatorium) is a term for a medical facility designed for long-term treatment and rest. 

# Historical Context
In America, sanitariums are most commonly associated with tuberculosis and mental asylums. Historically, working-class individuals who struggled with substance use were institutionalized either in prisons or state asylums without real programs or treatment. Wealthier individuals often chose private sanitariums to avoid the discomfort and public embarassment of psychiatric institutionalization. Although the facilities represented a shift towards seeing addiction as a medical problem rather than just a moral or spiritual failing, their solutions were experimental and often dangerous. Among the most notable of these networks was the Keeley Institutes.
