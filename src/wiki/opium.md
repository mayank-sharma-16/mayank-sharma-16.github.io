# Overview
Opium is a natural narcotic made from the seeds of the poppy plant, containing morphine, codeine, and thebaine as active alkaloids. It's the primary source from which other opiates are derived, including heroin. 

# Historical Context
Some of the earliest records of the poppy plant being cultivated date back to ancient Mesopotamia around 3400 BCE.
