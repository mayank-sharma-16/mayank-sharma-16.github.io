# Overview
The Sons of Temperance were a fraternal [[mutual-aid|mutual aid]] society founded to support total [[abstinence|abstinence]] from alcohol. At its peak in 1855, it had over 134,000 members and thousands of chapters, but the US Civil War disrupted national networks and local chapters mostly merged into other [[temperance|temperance]] or [[prohibitionism|Prohibition]] era organizations, like the Women's Christian Temperance Union (WCTU) and the Anti-Saloon League. The branches in the United Kingdom and Australia eventually became a life insurance provider. The Order of the Sons of the Temperance Friendly Society in Britain survived until 2012.
