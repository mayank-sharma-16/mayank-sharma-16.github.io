# Overview
The Special Action Office for Drug Abuse Prevention (SAODAP) was a temporary agency established under the Executive Office of the President in 1971 to centralize [[substance-use-disorder|substance use]] prevention, education, and treatment policies. Although it was scheduled to be terminated in 1975, some of its functions were transferred to the National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA) while others were recreated in the [[office-of-national-drug-control-policy|Office of National Drug Control Policy (ONDCP)]] in 1988.
