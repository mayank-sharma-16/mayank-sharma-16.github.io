# Overview
The Lester Research Group, or Lester Lab, is a neuroscience and bioengineering lab focused on [[addiction|addiction]] to [[nicotine|nicotine]]. Their work includes developing [[wearable-continuous-nicotine-monitors|wearable continuous nicotine monitors (CNMs)]], studying the [[pharmacokinetics|pharmacokinetics]] of nicotine, researching the "inside-out" [[psychoneuropharmacology|psychoneuropharmacology]] of the upregulation paradox, and developing [[neuroimaging|neuroimaging]] tools that use fluorescent biosensors to light up when nicotine is present.
