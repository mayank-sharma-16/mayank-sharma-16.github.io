# Overview
The Harvard University Health Services has roots going back to institutions from 1899, but its modern consolidation was completed in 1961. For substance use, their services include:

- Office of Alcohol & Other Drug Services, operated by the HUHS Center for Wellness and Health Promotion

- [[counseling-and-mental-health-services|Counseling and Mental Health Services (CAMHS)]] for [[confidentiality|confidential]] therapy that takes both [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] and [[abstinence|abstinence]]-based approaches

- clinician-led, abstinence-based support groups 

- consultations with clinicians to coordinate care with external specialists
