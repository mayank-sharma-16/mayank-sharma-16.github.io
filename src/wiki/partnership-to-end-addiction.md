# Overview
The Partnership to End Addiction is a national non-profit dedicated to recovery from substance use for youth and young adults by combining family support with systemic advocacy. The organization was founded as the Partnership for a Drug-Free America in 1985 and became famous for the "This Is Your Brain on Drugs" PSA. The current version of the organization was the result of a merger between the Partnership for Drug-Free Kids and Center on Addiction. Their projects include bilingual specialist helplines, [[peer-recovery|peer-to-peer]] coaching based on [[community-reinforcement-and-family-training|Community Reinforcement and Family Training (CRAFT)]], [[lobbying|lobbying]] the government for more resources for SUD treatment, campaigns to prevent youth vaping, and a podcast hosted by Elizabeth Vargas about destigmatizing addiction.
