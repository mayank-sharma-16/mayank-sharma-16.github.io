# Overview
The Center for Health Advocacy and Wellness (CHAW) at Florida State University was established in 2016 as a rebranding of the Health Promotion Department, to mark a shift between reactive and proactive health initiatives. They coordinate the Smart Choices programs like Brief Alcohol Screening and Intervention for College Students (BASCIS), distribution and education for [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]], and oversee a collegiate recovery program called Living Intentionally, Finding Togetherness (LIFT).
