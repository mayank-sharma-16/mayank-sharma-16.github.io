# Overview
Serenity is a popular concept in recovery circles that describes the inner peace and emotional stability that comes from letting go of the desire to control things outside of oneself. 

The Serenity Prayer is one of the most commonly repeated prayers in 12-step groups like Alcoholics Anonymous:

*"God, grant me the serenity to accept the things I cannot change, the courage to change the things I can, and the wisdom to know the difference."*

The original prayer was written by Reinhold Niebuhr, who wrote it in the context of enduring hardships and setbacks for those participating in social justice movements.
