# Overview
Adela-Maria Isvoranu is a clinical psychologist who uses network [[psychometry|psychometrics]] to study how [[alcohol|alcohol]], [[cannabis|cannabis]], and [[stimulant|stimulants]] impact symptoms of mental health like the effects of [[trauma|trauma]] and [[psychosis]]. Her research has connected substance use to clusters of symptoms mediated by anxiety and depression, as well as psychosocial difficulties like impulsivity, stress, and [[adverse-childhood-experience|adverse childhood experiences (ACEs)]]. Isvoranu's work has helped establish statistical standards used to model DSM-5 [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] criteria and treat the disorder as behaviors that loop into each other rather than latent underlying conditions.
