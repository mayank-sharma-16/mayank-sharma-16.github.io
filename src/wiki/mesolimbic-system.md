# Overview
The mesolimbic system, or mesolimbic pathway, is a neural circuit in the brain that regulates reward and motivation, incentive salience, and reinforcement learning. Addictive substances and processes are often described as "hijacking" this pathway with artificial surges in dopamine levels. Dysregulation of this circuit, as in cases of depression, can impact motivation or result in [[anhedonia|anhedonia]].
