# Overview
The Cambridge Health Alliance (CHA) is a medical provider and health system for over 140,000 residents, with an official affiliation with Harvard University. They operate the CHA Bridge Clinic as a walk-in outpatient center that does not require insurance and provides [[harm reduction]], counseling, wound care, [[recovery|recovery]] coaching, [[detoxification|detoxification]], and [[buphrenorphine|buprenorphine]] and [[naltrexone|naltrexone]] as Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT). Within the Department of Psychiatry is the Division on Addiction and Center for Mindfulness and Compassion, which have conducted clinical trials and training programs on [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]].
