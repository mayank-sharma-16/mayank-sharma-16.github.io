# Overview
Shatterproof is a national non-profit that addresses [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]] by promoting access to treatment and diffusing public stigma. They maintain a [[shatterproof-treatment-atlas|Treatment Atlas]] as an online platform to discover evidence-based SUD treatment facilities, run a National Stigma Initiative that involves educational modules for schools and workplaces, and host events like the [[shatterproof-walk-to-end-addiction|Shatterproof Walk to End Addiction Stigma]].
