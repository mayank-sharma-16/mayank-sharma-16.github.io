# Overview
The Center for Addiction and Pain Prevention and Intervention (CAPPI) at the University of Alabama at Birmingham is an academic and clinical research center that studies the intersection of chronic pain and [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]] with a strong emphasis on addressing the [[opioid-crisis|opioid crisis]]. They also host the [[alabama-summit-on-addiction-and-pain|Alabama Summit on Addiction and Pain]] every fall.
