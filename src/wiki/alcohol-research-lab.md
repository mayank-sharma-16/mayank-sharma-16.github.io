# Overview
The Alcohol Research Lab at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) is a specialized research group focused on the effects of alcohol consumption and the progression into Alcohol Use Disorder (AUD). Their studies include [[electroencephalogram|electroencephalogram (EEG)]] studies, biosensor tracking, automated text processing (Natural Language Processing), and other measurements that monitor brain activity to see the effects of social [[anxiety|anxiety]].
