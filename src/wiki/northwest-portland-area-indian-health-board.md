# Overview
The Northwest Portland Area Indian Health Board (NPAIHB) was established in 1972 as a non-profit organization serving 43 federally recognized tribes in Oregon, Washington, and Idaho. Their programs include the Northwest Tribal [[epidemiology|Epidemiology]] Center to conduct community health research, adolescent health through We R Native, suicide prevention through THRIVE, substance use programs, dementia care, and specialized training for building clinical capacity for SUD.
