# Overview
Redlining refers to the historic practice in the United States of grading neighborhoods as hazardous for the purpose of withholding resources, which generally maintained segregated neighborhoods by guiding banks to deny loans in communities of color. 

Studies show that neighborhoods that experienced redlining also experience some of the highest drug overdose rates. They're also more likely to have liquor stores and bodegas that sell alcohol, lack safe recreational spaces, face scarcities of resources for mental health care, and have limited access to healthy food options.
