# Overview
The National Institute of Mental Health defines a phobia as:

*an intense fear of—or aversion to—a specific object or situation. While anxiety is natural under some circumstances, people with phobias feel fear that is out of proportion to the actual danger presented by the situation or object.*

The DSM-5 offers three categories for phobias: Specific Phobias, Social Anxiety Disorder (Social Phobia), and Agoraphobia.
