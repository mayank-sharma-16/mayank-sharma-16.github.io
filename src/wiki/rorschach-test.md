# Overview
The Rorschach test is a widely known yet controversial assessment that asks clients to interpret inkblot cards to examine their personality, thoughts, and feelings. Today, though it's far less commonly used than before, a standard modern version exists called the Rorschach Performance Assessment System (R-PAS). Studies show that certain aspects of the Rorschach test can help identify schizophrenia and bipolar disorder, though the risk of "false positives" from healthy individuals with subjectively varied interpretations is also high. There also hasn't been much evidence to support the theoretical premise of the test, which is that encountering ambiguous stimuli forces a projection from the unconscious.
