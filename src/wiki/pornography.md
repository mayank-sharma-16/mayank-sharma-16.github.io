# Overview
Pornography refers to sexually explicit material that is intended to be sexually provocative or arousing. 

Whether pornography is addictive or otherwise harmful for mental health remains controversial, but the medical establishment does not, as a consensus, recognize porn addiction as a disease or disorder itself. Neither the DSM-5 nor the ICD-11 mention a specific "pornography addiction," though the ICD-11 does include Compulsive Sexual Behavior Disorder (CSBD) as an impulse-control disorder. Researchers often argue that porn use that interferes with daily life should be seen as a maladaptive coping mechanism for stress rather than a specific disorder, and they also argue that much of the psychological distress surrounding pornography use comes from internalized religious or moral conflicts. Still, there are Porn Addicts Anonymous (PAA) groups for those who wish to have peer support in maintaining abstinence from any and all usage of porn. [^0]
