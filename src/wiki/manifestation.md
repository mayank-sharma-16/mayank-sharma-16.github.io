# Overview
"Manifestation" means the act of bringing something into existence. 

In spiritual circles that overlap with recovery and wellness circles, the term is used to refer to using one's beliefs or intentions to shape reality. The [[law-of-attraction|law of attraction]] is one spiritual principle that believes positive thinking and visualization is key to manifesting positive outcomes in reality, whereas negative thinking will manifest in negative outcomes. The idea of changing reality through the mind alone is firmly in the realm of superstition, but there are some studies that show that positive mindsets can improve problem-solving skills and confidence by leading to greater perceived rewards for the application of effort.
