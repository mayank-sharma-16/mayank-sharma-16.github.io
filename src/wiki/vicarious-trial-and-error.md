# Overview
Vicarious trial and error (VTE) is a pattern where an organism pauses to examine alternative options before making a decision. That can include a rat in a maze or a student on a multiple-choice exam. VTE appears most often in the early stages of learning or when facing high-stakes or complex decisions. The more a pattern becomes learned as a habit, the less VTE is observed. 

Chronic substance use affects the VTE in different waits depending on the type of substance used. Those with a history of chronic [[cocaine|cocaine]] use showed lowered VTE behavior when looking at high-cost low-reward options, meaning their ability to deliberate before making a choice is disrupted. Those with a history of chronic morphine use showed the same VTE as non-users, but they struggle to change their minds and re-evaluate if the cost turns out to be unacceptably high.
