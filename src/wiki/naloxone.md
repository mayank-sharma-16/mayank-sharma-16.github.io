# Overview
Naloxone is the generic name of a medication that reverses opioid [[overdose|overdoses]]. Narcan is the brand name for the same medication, just prepackaged as a nasal spray available [[over-the-counter|over-the-counter (OTC)]]. It works as an opioid [[antagonist|antagonist]] that attach to opioid receptors in the brain to block the molecules of a substance from reacting with the body, which restores normal breathing in the case of an overdose. It's also safe to take in the case that someone is given the medication but doesn't have opioids in their body. Although it's considered extremely effective at reversing overdoses, the [[half-life|half-life]] for naloxone is much shorter than that of opioids like [[fentanyl|fentanyl]], which means that someone can slip back into an overdose after it's been reversed - a process called [[re-narcotization|re-narcotization]].
