# Overview
Peyote is a small cactus that contains over 50 alkaloids including [[mescaline|mescaline]], which produces [[psychedelic|psychedelic]] effects. It has been used for thousands of years by indigenous tribes throughout North America and continues to be used by the Native American Church. Outside of the Church's exemption for religious purposes, peyote is a Schedule I controlled substance in the United States.
