# Overview
NEUROSEC (Neuroscience, Ethics and Society) is a research group under the Department of Psychiatry at the University of Oxford. Its key initiatives include:

- NeurOx Young People's Advisory Group (YPAG): a cohort aged 14-25 who produce research projects on the intersection of ethical frameworks and youth mental health

- Design Bioethics Lab (DBL): an initiative building tools to explore moral attitudes and public concerns about health issues

- Oxford Winter Neuroethics School (OWNS): an annual training program for students, professionals, and researchers to explore state-of-the-art [[neuroethics|neuroethics]]
