# Overview
The Comprehensive Center for Pain & Addiction (CCPA) at the University of Arizona was founded in 2020 as a research and clinical hub to address the intersection between chronic pain and substance use, particularly in the wake of the [[opioid-crisis|opioid crisis]] and the demand for non-addictive medications to manage pain. Their services include workforce training like a minor degree in [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]], [[harm-reduction|harm-reduction]] advocacy for treatments like [[buprenorphine|buprenorphine]], and facilitation of clinical trials like direct transcranial stimulation.
