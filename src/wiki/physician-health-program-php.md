# Overview
Physician Health Programs (PHPs) are organizations that support doctors and other healthcare professionals with mental health conditions, including substance use. The Federation of State Physician Health Programs (FSPHP) supports PHPs in almost every state; California is a notable exception in its lack of state-run PHPs. Substance use disorders (SUDs) suffer from a lot of stigma, but medical professionals are under especially heavy pressure to not report health issues. There's often an idealized expectation that health professionals are free of any health condition themselves.
