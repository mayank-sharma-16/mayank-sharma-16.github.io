# Overview
Founded in 2011 by Paul Bayes Kitcher and Claire Morris, Fallen Angels Dance Theatre is a dance company in the United Kingdom dedicated to promoting creative expression among dancers in [[recovery|recovery]]. According to their website, they are the only company in the UK with their mission. Their regular sessions involve [[grounding|grounding]] techniques, [[breathwork|breathwork]], and improvisation to release feelings of fear, [[ego|ego]] inflation, and isolation.
