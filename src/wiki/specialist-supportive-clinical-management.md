# Overview
Specialist Supportive Clinical Management (SSCM) is a treatment framework designed for [[anorexia-nervosa|anorexia nervosa]] that combines clinical management with supportive psychotherapy. Rather than challenging the [[cognitive-distortion|irrational beliefs]] that prevent healthy eating with personal experimental evidence, as Enhanced Cognitive-Behavior Therapy (CBT-E) would, SSCM treats eating as a medical prescription while providing a validating space to discuss the coping skills needed to maintain progress. A premise of SSCM is that a brain that has undergone an anorexic period is malnourished and needs to be physically restored before it can effectively update its beliefs. Studies show that SSCM's effectiveness is comparable to or even greater than CBT-E.
