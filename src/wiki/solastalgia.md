# Overview
Solastalgia is the distress caused when someone's home environment changes while they're still inhabiting it. 

It was coined in 2003 by Glenn Albrecht in the context of environmental philosophy and natural disasters spurred on by climate change. Farmers, ranchers, indigenous populations, and rural communities in particular have strong personal, emotional, or religious ties to specific landscapes and can't just relocate an entire network of people and cultural institutions easily.
