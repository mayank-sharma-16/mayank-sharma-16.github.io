# Overview
The University of Chicago Medicine Behavioral & Substance Addiction Clinic operates under the Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Neuroscience as a clinical [[recovery|recovery]] program. Their offerings include [[confidentiality|confidential]] screenings, medication-assisted therapy (MAT), managing co-occurring conditions like [[depression|depression]] and [[anxiety|anxiety]], and individual and group therapy sessions often rooted in [[cognitive-behavioral-therapy|Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)]].
