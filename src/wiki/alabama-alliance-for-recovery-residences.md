# Overview
The Alabama Alliance for Recovery Residences (AARR) is a nonprofit that certifies [[sober-living-environment|sober living homes]] and [[recovery|recovery]] housing for the state of Alabama. It serves as a state affiliate of the National Alliance for Recovery Residences (NARR) and partners with the Alabama Department of Mental Health (ADMH). They publish a directory of certified homes, conduct workshops to teach housing operators about best practices, and advocates for supporting recovery infrastructure to policymakers and politicians.
