# Overview
The Oregon Health Authority (OHA) was established in 2009 by the Oregon Assembly in House Bill 2116 to centralize the state's public health programs under a single agency. It addresses [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorder (SUD)]] through funding [[detoxification|detox programs]], outpatient counseling, medication-assisted treatment (MAT) like methadone and buprenorphine, [[peer-recovery|peer support]], housing assistance, [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]], [[syringe-services-program|syringe service programs (SSPs)]], [[overdose|overdose]] surveillance, short-term stays in residential treatment facilities, and others.
