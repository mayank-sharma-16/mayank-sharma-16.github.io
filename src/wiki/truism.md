# Overview
Truisms are statements that are so obviously true that it's often implied they're not worth mentioning.

Recovery spaces have a lot of phrases that many consider to be truisms:

- "Everything happens for a reason": whether it does or it doesn't, someone still has to process their emotions and suffering related to it, so this can push someone into intellectualizing events while repressing their emotions or systemic challenges

- "It is what it is": this phrase might intend to encourage acceptance of a situation, but it can also be used dismissively towards problem-solving or objections to toxic behaviors that cross boundaries

- "Your best thinking got you here": while it might be true that someone's existing pattern of behaviors has to change and this phrase calls out [[denial|denial]], someone also uses their best thinking when they decide to follow other people's advice. Their best thinking also gets them out. However, the implication of this phrase is that someone should let go of their own rational judgment and comply with something despite their skepticism.
