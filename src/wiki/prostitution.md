# Overview
Prostitution refers to the exchange of sexual services for some type of compensation. Throughout most of the United States, prostitution is illegal, except for a few licensed institutions in Nevada. That doesn't stop a black market from thriving though. 

84% of street-based sex workers report issues with substance use. 50% of women and 18.5% of men in clinical treatment for substance use disorders (SUDs) have engaged in prostitution at some point in their life. These rates are driven by two patterns: prostitution is a way to pay for substances for those with SUD, and those who engage in prostitution are more likely to develop SUD when trying to cope with the traumas of sex work. In cases of human trafficking, victims are often coerced into substance use so that a dependency can be used against them as a method of compliance.
