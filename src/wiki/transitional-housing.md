# Overview
Transitional housing programs provide temporary housing to vulnerable individuals, typically coupled with specialized social and medical services. Vulnerable groups include those who had been homeless, experienced injuries and disabilities, or had mental health conditions. Stays generally last up to 2 years based on guidelines from the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD). Specialized services include case management, vocational counseling, job placement, recovery programs, and mental health services.
