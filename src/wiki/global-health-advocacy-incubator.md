# Overview
The Global Health Advocacy Incubator (GHAI) is a non-profit with civil society organizations (CSOs) in over 40 countries, with its core financial backing from Bloomberg Philanthropies.

GHAI manages an Overdose Prevention Initiative to reduce barriers between individuals and care, particularly in bureaucratic environments. They were part of the coalition that advocated for the passage of the Mainstreaming Addiction Treatment (MAT) Act which increased the number of clinicians who can prescribe buprenorphine for [[opioid-use-disorder|Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)]]. They also worked to secure the reauthorization of the SUPPORT Act, which funded opioid recovery centers, [[peer-recovery|peer support training]], residential treatment for pregnant women, and loan repayments for treatment providers. In partnership with the National Safety Council, they pushed OSHA to issue official workplace guidance about stocking [[naloxone|Naloxone (Narcan)]] to reverse an opioid [[overdose|overdose]]. Lastly, they push the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to protect the access to healthcare coverage from those with [[substance-use-disorder|Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)]].
