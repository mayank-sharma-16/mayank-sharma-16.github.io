# Overview
AddictionCenter.com is owned by Recovery Worldwide, LLC, a marketing firm based in Florida, USA that specializes in treatment for substance use, which makes the source a private advertising directory that does not endorse or provide advice regarding the items in its listings, though some of their content does make use of third-party medical professionals for expert review and they often aggregate data from third-party repositories like the CDC and SAMHSA.
