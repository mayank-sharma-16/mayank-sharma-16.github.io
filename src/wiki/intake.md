# Overview
In medical contexts, intake refers to the processes that bring onboard someone as a client or patient into a health service. 

# Components

- [[biopsychosocial|Biopsychosocial]] assessment

- Diagnostic evaluation

- Discovery & onboarding summary (non-clinical)

- Clinical Formulation (4Ps: predisposing, precipitating, perpetuating, protective factors)

# Assessments

- Substance USE & ASAM Criteria Assessment

- Trauma & Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) Summary

- Risk, Safety & Lethality Assessment

- Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) Screener Summary

- Cultural Formulation Interview (CFI)

- PHQ-9

- GAD-7

- PCL-5

- AUDIT/DAST-10

- Strengths, Assets, & Protective Factors Inventory

- Functional Impairment Matrix
