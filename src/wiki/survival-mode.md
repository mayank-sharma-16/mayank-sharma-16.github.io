# Overview
"Survival mode" is an informal term that refers to a state of intense stress and [[anxiety|anxiety]], which might be chronic, and includes symptoms like hypervigilance, insomnia, and elevated heart rate and blood pressure. The opposite of survival mode is often called "thrive mode." 

In mental health recovery circles, many maladaptive coping mechanisms were, at one point, adaptive patterns learned from a survival mode mindset in response to real trauma or distress. Healing can require getting to a place where those survival mode responses can be unlearned through a safe and validating environment. For instance, someone who procrastinates might actually be living in a survival mode where they just focus on getting through the next hour. This mindset might have helped in an environment that called for them to stay hypervigilant instead of thinking in terms of the long-run, but it might be maladaptive at work or school where consistently visible progress is important to demonstrate.
