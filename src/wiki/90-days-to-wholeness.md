# Overview
90 Days to Wholeness: A Porn Addiction Recovery Devotional and Coloring Journal for Women (2015) is a book by Crystal Renaud Day, a Kansas City minister and certified [[recovery|recovery]] coach who owns Living on Purpose Coaching & Pastoral Counseling.[^0]

Loosely based on the [[twelve-step|12-step]] tradition of attending 90 meetings in 90 days in early sobriety, the book covers her 90 Days to Wholeness plan that combines daily scripture and journaling prompts with her S.C.A.R.S Recovery Process to help women struggling with addictions (primarily sexual [[compulsion|compulsions]] like [[pornography|pornography]]): 

- Surrender

- Confession

- Accountability

- Responsibility

- Sharing

A free interactive version of the book's 90 day plan, adapted from Renaud Day's earlier book [[dirty-girls-come-clean|Dirty Girls Come Clean (2011)]], exists on Bible.com[^1]
