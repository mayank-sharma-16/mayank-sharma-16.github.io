# Overview
From performances inside San Quentin State Prison to performing for President Gerald Ford and President Donald Trump (then just a businessman hosting galas), Mark Lundholm has made a comedy career from the lessons learned through his past struggles with addiction and dysfunctional relationships.

# Commentary
I once called him a "fucktard" to his face, and then I got a free copy of his book *Shortcuts: Better Ways to Better Days*. Nice guy.
