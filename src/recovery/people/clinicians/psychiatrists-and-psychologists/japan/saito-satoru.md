---
title: Saito Satoru
---

In a 1985 book, Satoru argued that a person with alcoholism was in some sense reborn as part of an underlying family problem, and the stress of a birth that isn't seen as a family affair can exacerbate problems. This included addressing family dynamics that could be dysfunctional, since an alcoholic simply resuming their past role while being sober could lead to someone else developing a mental illness because of the stress.
