# Commentary

Volkow is the great-granddaughter of Leon Trotsky. I'm not shitting you even a little bit. She grew up in the house where he was assassinated with an icepick. Trotsky's granddaughter now heads an organization under the United States federal government that funds more than 85% of the world's research of the health consequences of drug abuse and addiction. It's a small world, isn't it?
