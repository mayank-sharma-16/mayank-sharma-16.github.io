# Overview
Dionysus is the Ancient Greek god associated with alcohol and celebratory festivities. 

# History and Etymology
In Ancient Greece, many believed that drinking was literally allowing the consciousness of Dionysus to possess one's body and mind. 

This motif has been popular in Latin-inspired cultures. *Los Borrachos* is a painting that was originally called "The Triumph of Dionysus."
