# Overview
Hypoarousal is a state in which the nervous system shuts down to conserve energy in response to prolonged or intense stress and trauma. This is in contrast to [[hyperarousal|hyperarousal]], though notably, a stressed nervous system may swing abruptly from one to the other. Signs of hypoarousal include:

- Emotional numbness

- Low energy or chronic fatigue

- Brain fog or trouble concentrating

- Dissociation
