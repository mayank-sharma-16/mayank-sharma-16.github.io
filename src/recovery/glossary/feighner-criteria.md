# Overview
In 1972, researchers at Washington University led by John Feighner proposed a set of diagnostic rules to be operationalized for psychologists and psychiatrists:

1. Primary Affective Disorders, divided into [[depression|depression]] and mania

2. Seconday Affective Disorders, like depression resulting from another disorder or condition

3. Schizophrenia

4. Anxiety Neurosis

5. Obsessive-Compulsive Neurosis

6. Phobic Neurosis

7. Hysteria 

8. Antisocial Personality Disorder

9. Alcoholism

10. Drug Dependence

11. Mental Retardation

12. Anorexia Nervosa

13. Homosexuality

14. Undiagnosed Psychiatric Illness, for when the other categories fail to describe someone's condition or they don't strictly meet the [[explication|explicated]] criteria
