# Overview
In clinical settings, black-and-white thinking refers to a type of cognitive distortion where someone thinks in extremes or polar opposites. For example, they might define success on an exam by achieving 100% and anything less to be a failure, or they might go between wanting to break up with a significant other to putting them on a pedestal. 

In the context of substance use disorders, black-and-white thinking can manifest as an [[abstinence-violation-effect|Abstinence Violation Effect (AVE)]] which can lead someone who deviates from their standards of abstinence to spiral much harder. "I messed up, so fuck it, I give up" is a very common thought preceding one of these relapses, whereas sometimes people can recognize that they don't have to let a night of drinking become a whole bender.
