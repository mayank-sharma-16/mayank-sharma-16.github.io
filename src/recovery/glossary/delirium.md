# Overview
Delirium is a sudden onset of confusion and decline in cognitive function as a result of brain disturbances. The [[diagnostic-and-statistical-manual-of-mental-disorders|Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-5-TR)]] lays out criteria to identify a state of delirium:

- Disturbance in attention and awareness

- Acute onsent and fluctuating course

- Cognitive deficits such as problems with memory, orientation, language or perception

- Direct physiological cause, including substance use or withdrawal

Some studies suggest that up to 60% of cases of delirium go unrecognized, so several screening instruments have been developed to help flag it:

- Confusion Assessment Method (CAM)

- 4 A's Test

- CAM-ICU/ICDSC
