# Overview
Delirium Tremens (DTs) are among the most dangerous and severe symptoms to emerge in withdrawals from alcohol. They can begin 2 to 4 days after someone has their last drink, and it can persist as long as 8 days. It includes:

- Confusion

- Hallucinations

- Diaphoresis (intense sweating)

- Tachycardia (elevated heart rate)

- High Blood Pressure

- Fever

- Body Tremors

- Seizures
