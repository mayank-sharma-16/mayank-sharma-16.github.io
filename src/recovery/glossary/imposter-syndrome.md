# Overview
Imposter syndrome is not an official medical diagnosis, but it was coined by clinical psychologists to describe the specific anxieties where people continually doubt themselves and feel like a fraud even when experiencing undeniable success. This can manifest as low self-esteem or self-worth, rationalizations that success was the result of luck, worrying about how others perceive one's competence, and perfectionism that sets up impossible standards.
