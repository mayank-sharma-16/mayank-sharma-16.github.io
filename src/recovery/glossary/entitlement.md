# Overview
"Entitlement" has at least two distinct uses depending on the context. 

For healthcare providers and insurance, "entitlement" refers to a legal right to receive healthcare benefits or coverage once an individual meets criteria. 

Within psychology and psychiatry, entitlement can be interpreted as a personality trait or a [[cognitive-distortion|cognitive distortion]] that presents with personality disorders:

- Narcissistic Personality Disorder (NPD): entitlement can be seen in an individual's constant need for admiration, validation, and/or rewards from others

- Antisocial Personality Disorder (ASPD): entitlement can be a factor in one's belief that they can violate the rights of others or break the rules for personal gain

- Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD): during emotional crises, people with BPD might feel entitled to ask for reassurance or support that breaks boundaries in response to intense fears surrounding the possibility of abandonment or rejection
