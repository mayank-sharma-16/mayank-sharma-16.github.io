# Overview
"Expected value" is a statistical construct that estimates the value of something by multiplying it by the probability of it happening. 

For example, if you want to buy a pack of random Pokemon cards where the chance of getting a card worth $50 is 2% and rest are worthless, the expected value of the card pack is $50 * 0.02 = $1. If you're being charged $10 for it, you can reason that it's not worth the price. You would need a 20% chance of pulling a $50 card in order to reach an expected value of $10 and have it be worth your while.

Expected value allows us to factor risk and uncertainty into our calculations about costs and benefits.
