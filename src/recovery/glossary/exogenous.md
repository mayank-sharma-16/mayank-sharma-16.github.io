# Overview
"Exogenous" refers to biochemical factors that originate or operate outside of a given system, in contrast to [[endogenous|endogenous]] factors. Bacteria, drugs, and radiation are all considered examples of exogenous factors to the human body that can act to change its state and relative health. In the world of [[designer-drugs|designer-drugs]], the goal is often to produce a product that, despite being exogenous to the body, operates like endogenous compounds. For instance, the phytocannabinoids of [[cannabis|cannabis]] are exogenous to the human body but function like endogenous cannabinoids, or [[endocannabinoids|endocannabinoids]], in order to deliver feelings of pleasure and eliminate feelings of anxiety.
