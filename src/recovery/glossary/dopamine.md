---
title: Dopamine
---

*"Dopamine is an enormously important molecule in addiction, central to th experience of learning and reward prediction, but its functioning is far more complicated than it is commonly portrayed. In a way, the popular stories about dopamine still rely on a circa-1980s level of understanding... For one thing, dopamine is not a 'pleasure' molecule: it has more to do with the feelings of desire and 'wanting,' not enjoyment and 'liking'"*

Roy Wise, the neuroscientist made famous for identifying dopamine as the pleasure molecule in the wake of the American 1980s crack epidemics, actually retracted the notion that dopamine was a pleasure molecule after more experiments in the 1990s.
