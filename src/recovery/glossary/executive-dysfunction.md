# Overview
Executive dysfunction is a symptom that presents as disruption to the brain's ability to manage information, create plans, and regulate emotions. It's unfortunately all-too-common for executive dysfunction to be framed as a [[character-defect|"character defect"]] like laziness, flakiness and inconsistency, or poor time management skills. Underlying disorders or conditions that might be present when observing executive dysfunction include:

- [[attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder|Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)]]

- [[autism-spectrum-disorder|Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)]]

- [[depression|Depression]]

- Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)

- Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

- Anxiety Disorders

- Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

- Neurodegenerative diseases like Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, Huntingon's, and frontotemporal dementia

- Chronic stress

- Substance use, including Post-Acut Withdrawal Symptoms (PAWS)

- Nutritional deficiencies
