# Overview
Evidence-based interventions (EBIs) are treatments and programs whose results have been validated through scientific research.

These can include therapies like:

- Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

- Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)

- Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT)

- Emotion-Focused Therapy (EFT)

- Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)

- Relational-Cultural Therapy (RCT)

- Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy (REBT)

- Behavior Activation (BA)

- Functional Analytic Psychotherapy (FAP)

- Compassion-Focused Therapy (CFT)

- Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR)

- Prolonged Exposure Therapy (PE)

- Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT)

- Somatosensory Psychotherapy

- Narrative Exposure Therapy (NET)

- Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)

- Habit Reversal Training (HRT)

- Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT)

- Multisystemic Therapy (MST)

- Functional Family Therapy (FFT)

- Positive Parenting Program (Triple P)

- Motivational Interviewing (MI)

- Contingency Management (CM)

- Community Reinforcement Approach (CRA)

- Relapse Prevention Therapy (RPT)

These can also include programs and partnerships that operate within a community:

- Housing First

- Critical Time Intervention (CTI)

- Nurse-Family Partnership (NFT)

- Syringe Services Programs (SSPs)
