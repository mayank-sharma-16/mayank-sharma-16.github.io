# Overview
Decriminalization generally refers to movements or policies that argue for undoing policies that treat substance use as a criminal activity, often by regulating substances under a harm reduction framework that puts users into treatment instead of incarceration. In the United States, decriminalization is seen as a movement in the opposite direction of the War on Drugs, which attempted to address substance use through militarized law enforcement crackdowns on substance users and dealers.
