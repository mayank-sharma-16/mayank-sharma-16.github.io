# Overview
Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) is a term that describes the arguments that large corporations have become one of the dominant structures of social organization and so they are obligated to be aligned with the social values of the communities they operate in.

Employee Assistance Programs (EAPs) can be argued to be a form of CSR. Although employers benefit from retaining a productive employee who might struggle with their substance use, including EAPs and addiction treatment within health insurance plans also goes a long way to legitimizing substance use disorders as a health issue within public perception.
