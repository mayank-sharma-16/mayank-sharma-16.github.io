# Overview
Methocarbamol is used as a muscle relaxant and pain reliever for health conditions that produce painful muscle spasms or cramps.

# Commentary
Anecdotally, this didn't too much for me when taking it for the soreness following motor tics, though it did seem to provide a slight comforting tingling at a very conservative dose, well under the standard starting dosages.
