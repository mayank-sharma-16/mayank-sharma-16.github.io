# Overview
Epidemiology is the field that concerns the spread of health determinants and distributions across populations, generally with the goal of influencing public health policy. This extends beyond infectious diseases and environmental contamination to trends in obesity and stress. Studies show that weight gain actually spreads across populations, implying that even something as fundamental as diet has psychosocial dimensions independent of individual factors or socioeconomic circumstances.
