# Overview
"Dipsomania" is an outdated term to describe habitual compulsions to drink alcohol. 

# History and Etymology
In the mid-19th century Victorian era, the term arose into prominence among physicians and psychiatrists as part of other monomanic compulsions like pyromania, kleptomania, and nymphomania, which put it into a pathologized and medicalized category of human behavior rather than a simple moral or willpower issue. When Magnus Huss introduced the term [[alcoholism|"alcoholism"]], the two terms captured different aspects of what we now call "alcohol use disorder." Alcoholism referred to chronic daily drinking while dipsomania referred to binge-drinking episodes.
