# Overview
Dual diagnosis treatment programs are iterations of substance use and mental health programs that acknowledge the co-occurrence of multiple mental health conditions and disorders alongside substance us. The relationship is bidirectional: the [[self-medication-hypothesis|self-medication hypothesis]] claims that people use substances as a way of addressing underlying health issues that could be a source of emotional or mental dysregulation, and substance use disorders can be a factor in exacerbating depression and anxiety.
