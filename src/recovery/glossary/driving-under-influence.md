# Overview
Driving Under Influence (DUI) is one of the most common legal issues encountered as a result of substance use. Approximately 30% of all traffic fatalities in the United States involved alcohol-impaired driving. A [[blood-alcohol-concentration|BAC]] of 0.08 is associated with a four times higher chance of a crash, and a BAC of 0.15 is 12 times more likely to lead to a crash.
