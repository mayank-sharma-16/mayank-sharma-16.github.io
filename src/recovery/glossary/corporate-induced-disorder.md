# Overview
"Corporate-induced disorder" is a slang phrase that emerged to describe anxiety, depression, and burnout that results in part from exposure to unhealthy workplace environments commonly found in 21st century bureaucratic corporations. Other related terms include "Corporate Traumatic Stress Disorder" (CTSD) and "Corporate Stockholm Syndrome," which earned an article in Psychology Today. [^0]
