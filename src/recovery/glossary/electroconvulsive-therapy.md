# Overview
Electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) is a medical procedure that runs electrical currents to the brain and triggers seizures for mental health conditions that are otherwise treatment-resistant. This stimulates a kind of reset in the brain that can break toxic patterns without needing weeks or months like antidepressants and therapeutic interventions.
