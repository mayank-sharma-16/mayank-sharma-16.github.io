# Overview
Cluster B personality traits makes it difficult for someone to control their emotions and maintain stable, healthy relationships. There are four personality disorders recognized by the [[diagnostic-and-statistical-manual-of-mental-disorders|DSM-5]]:

- Antisocial Personality Disorder, characterized by a pattern of disregard for the rights of others and a lack of empathy after manipulation

- Borderline Personality Disorder, involving intense mood swings surrounding a fear of abandonment and feelings of emptiness

- Histrionic Personality Disorder, defined by a strong need to be the center of attention through overly dramatic actions or their physical appearance 

- Narcissistic Personality Disorder, in which someone has an inflated sense of self-importance with a desire for attention and praise that disregards the feelings of others
