# Overview
Dimethyltryptamine (DMT) is a natural psychedelic compound, often found in plants such as those used for Amazonian ayahuasca brews. Depending on how it's taken, it can last up to 30 minutes or, if taken orally with an MAOI inhibitor, several hours.
