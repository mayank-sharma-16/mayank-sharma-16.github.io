# Overview
"Character defects" is a phrase that comes up often in recovery circles that are based around a 12-step model of facilitating a spiritual awakening, in part through moral introspection of personal wrongdoings. The term is considered interchangeable with "shortcomings." The website for the AA of Cleveland umbrella organization enumerates some typical ones:

- Resentment

- Anger

- Fear

- Cowardice

- Self-pity

- Self-justification

- Egotism

- Self-condemnation

- Guilt

- Dishonesty

- Evasiveness

- Impatience

- Hate

- False Pride

- Denial

- Jealousy

- Envy

- Laziness

- Procrastination

- Insincerity

- Negative Thinking

- Immoral Thinking

- Perfectionism

- Intolerance

- Criticism

- Gossip

- Greed [^0]

# Commentary
Although 12-step models call substance use disorders a disease, the steps heavily weave moral threads into the medical model. Personally, while I think self-reflection over one's moral values and making amends can be very helpful for some people, I generally oppose the idea of moralizing disorders, especially when there are physiological and social components to them.
