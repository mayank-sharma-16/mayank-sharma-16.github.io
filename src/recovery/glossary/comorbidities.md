# Overview
A comorbidity is a medical condition that occurs alongside another condition. For instance, diabetes and hypertension are common comorbidities, as are depression and anxiety. Sometimes the conditions directly related through the mechanisms they operate through, where one is caused by the other or they share underlying dysfunctions. It's also possible for them to just have shared risk factors like poor diet with high cholesterol or smoking. Sometimes they're called co-occurring disorders instead.
