# Overview
Acute Stress Disorder (ASD) is a short-term mental health condition that generally develops in the first month after experiencing or learning about a traumatic event. Symptoms include anxiety, intrusive thoughts, avoidance, and dissociative symptoms that interfere with the activities of daily life.
