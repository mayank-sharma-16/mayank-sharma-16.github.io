Before 1914, it was federally legal for practically anyone to manufacture and sell opium, morphine, cocaine, and other drugs that are highly regulated today. There were a patchwork of local, state, and tribal regulations, but without the ability to regulate interstate commerce, it was easy enough to just order drugs through the mail from another state's pharmacy to skirt those regulations.
