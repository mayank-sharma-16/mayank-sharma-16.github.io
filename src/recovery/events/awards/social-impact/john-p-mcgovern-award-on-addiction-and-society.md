# Overview
Established in 1997, the John P. McGovern Award on Addiction and Society is presented to leaders who make significant contributions to public policy, addiction treatment, and research that shape the model of addiction used by society. An endowment from the John P. McGovern Foundation funds the award.

# Past Winners
- Petros Levounis (2024)

- Kelly J. Clark (2022)

- Margaret A. Jarvis (2021)

- John Renner (2018)

- Mark S. Gold (2015)
