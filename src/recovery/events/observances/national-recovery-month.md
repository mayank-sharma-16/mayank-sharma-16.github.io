# Overview
Led by the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA), the National Recovery Month is held every September. Studies have shown spikes in first-time drug use in students over the summer, so September serves as a chance to reach students who might need help at the start of a new school year. Each year has a new theme to give local advocacy groups a common focus point, such as "Recovery is For Everyone."
