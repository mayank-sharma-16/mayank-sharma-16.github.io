# Overview
The National Association of Addiction Treatment Providers (NAATP) hosts an NAATP Hill Day that brings treatment providers and advocates to Capitol Hill in Washington D.C. to meet with federal representatives. It comes at the end of the NAATP National Addiction Leadership Conference.
