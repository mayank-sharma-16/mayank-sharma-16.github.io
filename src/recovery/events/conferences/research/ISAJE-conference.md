# Overview
The International Society of Addiction Journal Editors (ISAJE) hosts an annual conference since they were formally established in 2001. While it began as a small event for academic editors to discuss issues and guidelines concerning academic publishing, it grew and adopted a hybrid format to all international participants to attend remotely. The physical conference changes its location each year as part of its mission to cultivate an international network.
