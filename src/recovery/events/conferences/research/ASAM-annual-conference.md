# Overview
The American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM) held its first annual educational conference in 1954, though it evolved from the New York City Medical Society on Alcoholism (NYCMSA) in 1967. It began as a regional meeting of physicians and grew into a four-day national symposium for professionals, clinicians, and researchers to collaborate on the latest developments in their spaces.
