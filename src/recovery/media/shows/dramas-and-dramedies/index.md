## Recently Modified

- [Severance](/recovery/media/shows/dramas-and-dramedies/severance)

I almost forgot that Mark is actually a functioning alcoholic in this show. One of the things that caught me off-guard was how Mark at work didn't seem to struggle with compulsive urges at all. It's not particularly unusual for functional alcoholics to make it through the workday without a drink or withdrawal symptoms. Mark at work definitely carries the physical effects of drinking from the…

- [The Sopranos](/recovery/media/shows/dramas-and-dramedies/the-sopranos)

First of all, this is the best show of all time. Many have tried to get me to shut up about it, and many have failed.

- [The Wire](/recovery/media/shows/dramas-and-dramedies/the-wire)

I like to think that The Wire is a systemic look at the themes The Sopranos explored psychologically. Both show a changing America at the cusp of the second millennium, though one is told through the orbit of a single person's actions and reactions while the other is told through a series of interweaving storylines. Both touch on the concept of change: evolution to some, decay to others.

- [The Boys](/recovery/media/shows/dramas-and-dramedies/the-boys)

I struggle to think of a show where my feelings about the first episode and last episode had as big a chasm as this one. Still, drugs and addiction are a part of it, so let's get this over it.
